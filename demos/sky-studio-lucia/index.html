Where Science Meets Spirituality

Sky Studio is a consciousness exploration space in Vancouver's Fairview neighbourhood. Founded by Tina Averback, the studio centres on the Lucia N°03 — a hypnagogic light device developed by Austrian neurologists that gently guides your brain into the space between waking and sleep.

What people see under the light — kaleidoscopic patterns, vivid colours, deep stillness — is unique to each session. The studio also offers sound healing, family constellations, and spinal energetics.

The Name

What You See Is Yours Alone

Kaleidoscopic geometry. Deep ocean blue. Infinite corridors of light. No two sessions are alike because the Lucia N°03 works with YOUR nervous system. The patterns, colours, and visions emerge from your own consciousness — the light just opens the door.

What We Offer

Experiences

Lucia Light Meditation

The signature experience. Lie back under the Lucia N°03 lamp and let flickering white light guide your brain into deep meditative states. Sessions are individual or small group.

Sound Healing

Crystal bowls, singing bowls, and overtone instruments create a bath of sound that melts tension and quiets the mind. No effort required — just rest and receive.

Constellations

Family and systemic constellations — a powerful modality for understanding hidden dynamics in relationships, health, and purpose. Group sessions and private work available.

Spinal Energetics

A gentle, intuitive approach to releasing stored tension and emotion through the spine. Combined with light therapy for deeper integration.
