About Us

Small Batches,
Big Flavours

Kid Sister's frozen delights are locally made and seasonally delicious. We make unique flavours of ice cream, sherbet, frozen yogurt, vegan sorbet, plus ice pops, fruit cream pops, and more.

Our ingredients include organic dairy, plus fruits and produce from family-owned farms right here on Vancouver Island. Every pint, pop, and scoop is crafted in small batches — so what's in the case today might not be there next week.

  • Organic dairy from local farms
  • Seasonal, small-batch recipes
  • Vegan & dairy-free options always available
  • Pints & half-pints to take home
"We believe the best frozen treats start with real ingredients and a little bit of love. Every batch is made by hand, right here in Victoria."
