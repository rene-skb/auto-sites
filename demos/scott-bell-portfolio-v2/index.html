How I Work
Find the structure underneath.
Then make it visible.
01
Understand the system
Every product has a hidden logic. I find it through research, mental models, and asking why until the real problem shows up.
02
Design with conviction
Strong opinions, loosely held. I take a position, prototype fast, test against reality, and iterate on evidence.
03
Ship and learn
Design systems, not one-offs. Everything I build creates leverage for the next thing. Speed comes from accumulated craft.

"Design as a way of making sense — and making things make sense."
