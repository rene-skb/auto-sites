What is bingsu?

Ice shaved fine enough to dissolve before you finish thinking about it.

Korean shaved ice — frozen slowly, shaved into ribbons thin enough to melt on contact. Topped with fresh mango, condensed milk, mochi, and almonds. Zoé brings this to downtown Montréal alongside Hong Kong desserts, mille crêpe cakes, and Presotea bubble tea. One space. All of it.

Zoé. A French name. A Korean-HK dessert café. In Montréal.

The name doesn't explain itself — it doesn't need to. You come in for bingsu and leave already planning when to come back for the mille crêpe.
