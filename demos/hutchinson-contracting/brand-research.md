# Brand research — Hutchinson Contracting

## Business
Hutchinson Contracting is a Greater Victoria general contractor focused on multi-family and townhome construction, commercial building projects, new residential construction, and strata/co-op refurbishments.

## Source-backed facts
- Office: 694 Sumas St., Victoria, BC.
- The official site emphasizes clear communication, structured project management, site supervisors, stakeholder communication, safety management, and quality control.
- Multi-family services include pre-construction planning, permitting, municipal coordination, project management, site supervision, multi-unit construction, finishing, turnover, and building-envelope work.
- Commercial services include office, retail, hospitality, industrial, healthcare, multi-use, educational, renovations, project management, and sustainable building solutions.
- One named testimonial is available: Dan Cox of Cox Developments on a complex seismic upgrade.
- Logos visible on the official site: Victoria Residential Builders Association, BBB Accredited Business, Canadian Home Builders Association.

## Brand direction
- Vibe: disciplined, construction-management-first, local, calm under complexity.
- Palette: warm paper, dark charcoal, cedar accent. The current site uses warm neutrals and construction imagery, so the redesign should feel structured rather than flashy.
- Typography: Sora for operational clarity, Source Serif 4 for senior editorial confidence. Different from recent Lucy contractor runs.
- Core angle: managed builds for many moving parts. The strongest real value is coordination for developers, strata councils, commercial owners, and homeowners.
