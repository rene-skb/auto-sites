# Sow Song — Brand Brief

*Custom heirloom jewelry by Tia Noelle Soby, Victoria BC*

## Brand Essence
Romantic nostalgia. Heirloom over trendy. Story over status. This is jewelry for people who want their ring to mean something — traces of history, marks of moments.

## The Story
- **Founded:** 2025 — "the year a long held dream became reality"
- **Founder:** Tia Noelle, Vancouver Island local
- **Previous career:** Florist (career pivot to jewelry)
- **Training:** Apprenticed with local mentors to learn metalsmithing
- **Studio:** Converted Travelux trailer — intimate, soulful space reflecting her Western roots
- **The name:** Honours her late grandfather — "Sow Song" was a tune he'd sing to the grandkids (banjo player)
- **Location:** Based in Nanaimo, BC; does consultations in Tofino

## Brand Manifesto (Her Exact Words)
> "Traces of history, marks of moments and cherished memories-in-the-making. Here we are everyday romantics, alchemists of our innermost feelings turned to gold."

> "Every piece is a love letter to the bonds, connections, and magic of life well lived—of life well-loved. From treasured friends and beloved life partners, to precious pets and soft self embraces, we pour our hearts into crafting poetry that lives with you throughout lifetimes."

> "Rooted on the coast, Sow Song sources continuous inspiration being among the trees and bodies of water. The wisdom of nature flows throughout their process and in turn each keepsake is as unique and individual as the ones who adorn them."

## Visual Identity

### Color Palette
- **Burgundy/Crimson** — Primary brand color. Their boxes, graphics, logo. Deep, warm red.
- **Cream/Warm White** — Background color on graphics. Not stark white — soft, aged, warm.
- **Gold** — The jewelry itself. Warm yellow gold, not cold white gold.
- **Soft blacks** — Used sparingly. Never harsh.

### Typography
- **Script** — Logo "Sow Song" in flowing burgundy script
- **Serif** — Body text. Something with character, not generic. Classic, slightly romantic.
- **Not modern sans-serif** — This isn't a tech brand. Avoid cold, clean, minimal.

### Photography Style
- Candlelit, golden hour, warm light
- Hands in nature — water, shells, stones, beaches
- Red jewelry boxes as recurring motif
- Tattooed hands (Tia herself)
- Vintage family photos (Grandpa Soby dedication)
- Red/burgundy cloth backgrounds for product shots
- Simple, spacious compositions — not busy, not cluttered

### Aesthetic Keywords
- Nostalgic
- Romantic
- Handmade
- Intimate
- Warm
- Heirloom
- Simple/minimalist (but not cold)
- Spaced out, breathing room

## The Grandpa Story (Touchstone) — USE THIS

**The name "Sow Song" is everything.** This is the emotional heart of the brand.

From her post (August 5, 2025):
> "Some things just stay with us. People, songs, scents, memories and so many more — my Grandfather was one of those things for me."
>
> "My Grandpa Soby was a musical man. Most of my memories of him are in of him in his garden or playing the banjo. He was such a warm, social person, and being around him always made things feel a little more sparkly. Sow Song was a tune he'd sing to us grandkids most times we were with him. Playful and full of love."
>
> "When I make jewelry, I'm always thinking about connection. I want the pieces I make to hold meaning throughout our lifetime. I like to think of them as tokens of love and some remind us of the people we care about, the way Sow Song reminds me of him."
>
> "When I was choosing a name for my jewelry brand, I wanted something organic, light, but still elegant and personal. Sow Song just felt like home and so meaningful to my heart."
>
> "My Grandpa passed when I was little, but his energy has honestly been with me throughout my life. I am beyond grateful for the connection we had and so happy to have a part of him in my jewellery journey now."
>
> "With Love Always"

From brand discovery process:
> "This process led us back to a central influence in Tia's life, her grandfather, whose wisdom, gentleness, and artistry formed a quiet thread through her own journey. Ultimately, this discovery became the seed of a name: *Sow Song*. A phrase that holds a multitude of meanings; of planting and singing, creating and cherishing, shaping and softening."

**Photo:** There's a vintage photo of Grandpa Soby with his banjo (Mike Soby). This MUST be on the site with the story.
