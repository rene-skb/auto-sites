Our Story

A family recipe,
a grandmother's kitchen

Tarboosh is a mother-and-son operation. Halah Abouhassan and her son Basil bring the flavours of their Egyptian heritage to the streets of Esquimalt, cooking everything from scratch: the sauces, the marinades, the falafel mix, even the pita.

"People will come here and say 'I just want to check your menu,' and I say, 'You know what? I'm going to feed you as well. You're on my door so I can't let you go.'"

"Tarboosh is the fez hat. It inspired me when I saw my grandpa wearing it as a uniform in the Egyptian military."

Halah Abouhassan, Owner
