About

The Owner Greets You Like You're Expected

The family behind Pho Vy is from Vietnam. They opened on Fort Street, and when the regulars kept coming back, they opened a second kitchen in Langford. Same recipes, same family, same welcome at the door.

There's a reason every Reddit thread asking "best pho in Victoria?" lands here. The broth simmers for twelve hours. The portions are generous. And the owner makes sure you leave full and happy. That's the whole strategy.

What to Order

The Regulars Know

01

Pho Vy Special #18

The signature bowl. Steak, flank, tendon, tripe, and meatballs in a twelve-hour bone broth. The one everyone orders first and keeps ordering. From $13.25.

02

Bun Bo Hue

Spicy beef noodle soup from central Vietnam. Lemongrass, chili oil, thick round noodles. Not pho — different tradition, same care.

03

Shrimp & Crab Noodle Soup

Seafood broth with shrimp and crab. Lighter than pho, just as satisfying. A regular favorite that doesn't get enough credit.

04

Spring Rolls & Salad Rolls

Crispy fried spring rolls with peanut sauce, or fresh pork salad rolls with herbs and rice paper. The perfect starter before your bowl arrives.
