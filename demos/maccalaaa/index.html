NTS Radio

Balearic to broken beat. Last Saturday of every month.

One Glove airs monthly on NTS, the independent broadcaster out of Dalston. The show moves between house, hip-hop, broken beat, balearic, and whatever Macca pulled from the crates that week. Two hours, no playlist, no rules.

The Host

Macca

DJ, NTS Radio host, and the person behind One Glove. Based in South London. Plays records at Carven fashion shows, Peckham basements, and a football pitch in Hackney with The Gun FC.

The show started because he had too many records and not enough people to play them to. That hasn't changed.
