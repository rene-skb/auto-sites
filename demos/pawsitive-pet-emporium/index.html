About

Thirty Years of Knowing What Scares Your Dog

"Pawsitive Pet Grooming is fantastic but were booked up for the year quite early — with a four-page waitlist when I called."

We opened in '95 when most of our current clients' dogs hadn't been born yet. Three decades of nails, baths, blowouts, lion cuts, and the occasional cat who'd rather be anywhere else.

We're also a pre-loved pet gear shop — collars, leashes, beds, toys. Gently used, locally sourced, rotating weekly. Local. Sustainable. Badass.

The Waitlist Is Four Pages Long. We've Never Run an Ad.

Dog parks, Reddit threads, that one friend whose poodle always looks incredible. Word of mouth is the only marketing we've ever needed.

Services

What We Do

01

Full Groom

Bath, blow dry, haircut, nail trim, ear clean. Every breed, every size. Especially good with anxious and reactive dogs — we take our time.

02

Cat Grooming

Lion cuts, mat removal, sanitary trims. Drop off, one hour, done. Some cats have been coming for years — Jenga's been getting her lion cut here for a decade.

Quick Touch-Ups

Nail clips, teeth brushing, ear cleaning, paw trims. Quick in-and-out service. Call ahead to confirm same-day availability.

Collars, leashes, beds, toys, carriers — gently used and priced right. Better for your wallet, better for the planet. New stock rotates weekly.
