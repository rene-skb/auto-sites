More Than Just Coffee

The Spiral has been a gathering place in the heart of Vic West for years — a warm, character-filled cafe right across from Banfield Park and the Y.

We're family-run and proud of it. Our coffee is organic and fair-trade. Our baked goods come fresh daily from Mosi's Bakery. And our walls feature rotating work from local artists, because community is what we're about.

With big windows, a hand-lettered menu board, a piano in the corner, and that unmistakable yellow wall — there's a reason people call us a hidden jewel.

Warm light through big windows, local art on the walls, the hum of conversation

Not a chain. Not a franchise.
Just a family-run cafe that cares.

Organic fair-trade coffee. Baked goods from Mosi's Bakery. Local art on the walls. Live music with no cover. This is what a neighbourhood cafe should feel like.

Live Music Nights

Singer-songwriters, folk acts, and local musicians fill the Spiral with live sound on Monday, Thursday, and Friday evenings. No cover charge — just come, grab a drink, and enjoy the show.

From intimate acoustic sets to full-on jam sessions, there's always something happening. Our piano gets plenty of use too.
