Inspired by Fruit,
Made with Love

Kid Sister started in 2010, born from a love of Mexican paleta culture and a belief that frozen treats should be made with real, honest ingredients.

Every batch is made from scratch in Victoria using organic dairy and fruit from local family farms. Visit us at our Esquimalt Road shop for scoops and pops, or find our pints and paletas at our mini outlet in Cook St Village. Seasonal flavours come and go quickly — that's part of the magic.
