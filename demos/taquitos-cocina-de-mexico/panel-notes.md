# Panel Notes — Taquitos Cocina de México

## WHY critique

**Steve Jobs — 7.8**
- The category truth is strong. Authentic Mexican food in Bedford is enough of a hook if you say it cleanly.
- The first hero version spent a little too much time explaining the broken website. That is support copy, not the main idea.
- Keep the emotional center on appetite plus earned credibility from people who know the food.

**Paul Graham — 7.4**
- The strongest sentence is the one about people from California and Los Angeles saying it tastes like home.
- Cut any line that sounds like commentary on the project itself instead of commentary on the restaurant.
- The page gets better when it sounds more literal.

**David Ogilvy — 8.0**
- “Bedford finally has the real thing” is clearer and more ownable than the earlier, cleverer line.
- Burritos, birria, churros, horchata, and tres leches are good specifics. Keep using actual dish names.
- Let the reviews do some of the selling instead of over-writing the setup.

**Panel average:** 7.73

**Next changes for Lucy**
1. Tighten the hero to a shorter, more direct conviction line.
2. Push the website problem down into supporting copy.
3. Keep naming dishes and using the California/LA proof because it is specific and human.

## WHAT critique

**Don Norman — 7.6**
- The page structure makes sense: why go, food, reviews, visit.
- The utility card in the hero is doing real work. Label it more literally.
- The ordering fallback should be obvious because the official site is currently unreliable.

**Steve Krug — 7.8**
- You mostly don’t make me think here. That’s good.
- The “What to order” section scans well because the names come first and the explanations are short.
- Trim any caption or note that repeats something users already know from the surrounding section.

**Jakob Nielsen — 7.2**
- Good practical paths: call, order online, Instagram.
- Make sure the rough state of the official domain is stated plainly once, not repeatedly.
- Hours should stay easy to spot near the top and in the visit section.

**Panel average:** 7.53

**Next changes for Lucy**
1. Rename soft labels into clearer utility language.
2. Reduce repeated explanation around the official site problem.
3. Keep the order links and hours highly visible.
