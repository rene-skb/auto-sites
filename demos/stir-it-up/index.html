The Story

One kitchen. One cook. Everything from scratch.

Stir It Up is Caribbean soul food the way it's supposed to taste. The roti is rolled by hand, the curry simmers all morning, and the jerk chicken sits in its marinade until it's ready. Nothing comes from a box.

The restaurant is tucked in an alley between Yates and Johnson, behind a green door covered in Caribbean art. You'll know you're close when the smell hits you.

The Menu

Every roti is rolled fresh. Every curry simmers all morning.

Curry Chicken Roti The one people drive across town for. Dhalpuri roti, rolled to order.
Jerk Chicken Scotch bonnet marinade, slow cooked. Served with rice and peas.
Doubles Bara and channa. A Trinidadian street food staple.
Jamaican Patties Flaky crust, seasoned filling. Beef or chicken.

The Name

Named after the Bob Marley song. The food is Caribbean. The love is real. The kitchen is small and the portions are not.
