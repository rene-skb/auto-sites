About

Made Here. Not Shipped Here.

Every batch of Kid Sister ice cream is made from scratch on Esquimalt Road. Organic dairy from local farms, fruit that's actually in season, and real flowers you can eat.

When the strawberries are gone, they're gone. When the dahlias bloom, they end up in a pop. That's how seasons work.

What We Make

Ice Cream Scoops

Hand-scooped small batches. Organic dairy, seasonal flavours, made that week.

Vegan Sorbet

Dairy-free and fruit-forward. Same care, same local ingredients.

Frozen Pops

Fruit and flower pops with edible petals frozen inside.
