About

The Miracle Worker on Oak Bay Ave

"I was turned away by another cobbler who told me my Doc Martens were a lost cause. Dave said if I wanted to come back, I could use his tools to work on them myself." Via Reddit · r/VictoriaBC

Dave has been repairing shoes at 2045 Oak Bay Avenue for over four decades. He's slightly gruff, deeply skilled, and will stay late to finish your boots if you need them. In a world of disposable everything, he's the reason your favourite pair lasts another ten years.

The shop sits across from the butcher, on the stretch of Oak Bay Ave where the village still feels like a village. No website. No app. Just bring your shoes in and Dave will tell you what he can do.
