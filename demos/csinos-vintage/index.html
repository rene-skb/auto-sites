The Story

Beautiful things have lives.
Those lives should continue.

Szandi grew up in Eastern Europe, where clothing wasn't disposable — it was inherited, repaired, passed on. When her family moved to Canada, thrift stores were how they built a home. That relationship with second-hand objects as something meaningful, not just affordable, never left her.

Csinos Vintage started in Fernwood in 2020 as an Instagram account and grew into a curated shop. The name says everything: the word means beautiful in Hungarian, and it describes both the pieces and the intention behind finding them.

"It's sort of a mood," Szandi says. "I'm not selling clothes. I'm selling a feeling."

The Name

Csinos.
Pronounced: chee-nosh.
Hungarian for beautiful.

It's the word her grandmother would use about a well-made dress. The word her mother used about a piece worth keeping. Now it's the word over the door — and the standard every item must meet before it comes inside.

What's Inside

Every piece was chosen for a reason.

Clothing

Dresses, blouses, trousers, knitwear — decades from the '60s to the '90s. Every garment was chosen for cut and quality, not just age. Nothing lands here by accident.

02

Shoes & Accessories

Household

Cheers Vintage Collective

Five women. One address. Five completely different eyes.

Csinos Vintage lives inside Cheers Vintage Collective at 770 Yates Street — five independent sellers, each with their own curated section. You're not choosing from one person's taste. You're in a room with five of them.

Every seller here started with Instagram, built a community, and earned a floor in the same building. The result is something no single shop could achieve alone.
