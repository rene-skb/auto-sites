The Shop

A warren of books. Since 1964.

Floor-to-ceiling shelves. A basement with more. Rare ephemera and the childhood classic you've been hunting for years. MacLeod's is not curated. It's everything, organized by people who've been doing this for 60 years.

Members of ABAC and ILAB: the antiquarian booksellers' professional associations. We buy, sell, and appraise. If what you have is worth something, we'll tell you what.

What's Here

Rare finds. Cheap classics. Everything in between.

We don't do a curated selection of 200 titles. We do the whole thing: floor to ceiling, basement included. No online catalog. Call ahead to check specific titles.

— Rare & Antiquarian First editions, out-of-print titles, valuable finds. We appraise. We know what we have, and we price accordingly.
— Indigenous Literature An intentional collection: Indigenous Canadian voices, histories, and perspectives, maintained and respected.
— Fiction & Literature A 1960s Lord of the Rings. Last year's Booker winner. Everything between. Usually a fraction of cover price.
— History & Non-Fiction Vancouver history, world events, science, philosophy. Deep shelves, deep subjects.
— Children's Books The ones you remember. The ones you want your kids to find. Priced to actually be given away.
— Art, Architecture & Design Monographs, catalogues, reference. Occasionally something rare surfaces. Come look.
