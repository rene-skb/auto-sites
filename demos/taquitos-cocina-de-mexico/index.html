Bedford finally has the real thing.

Taquitos Cocina de México serves burritos, birria, taquitos, churros, horchata, and tres leches at 12 Ella Lane in Bedford. People from California and Los Angeles keep saying it tastes like home, which is about as useful a review as this category gets.

Why people keep showing up

It already has a real menu, a real following, and enough word of mouth to make “best tacos in Halifax” show up in public reviews. The website just hasn’t caught up yet.

4.6 on Uber Eats, with 250+ ratings

Enough order volume to trust the signal, not just the first-week friends-and-family bump.

Burritos, taquitos, birria, churros, and tres leches all show up in the live menu

This is not a one-hit taco counter. The range is wide without feeling generic.

The room has become a gathering place too

Instagram shows karaoke nights, live performances, desserts, and regulars, not just a silent pickup shelf.

Food first, then the room around it

The photo set is messy in the honest way small restaurants usually are: promo graphics, dessert drops, kitchen shots, events, people. That still tells the truth better than fake polish.

What to order

The delivery menu gives us the cleanest view of what people actually buy. Start with the dishes that keep getting pulled to the top, then work outward.

Most liked Burritos

Your choice of pastor, tinga, cochinita, chorizo, fish, carne asada, or suadero in a 12-inch flour tortilla with rice, beans, lettuce, sour cream, mozza, and hot salsa.

Most liked Los Nachos

A shareable plate you can add tinga, carne asada, suadero, cochinita, or chorizo to, which is exactly the kind of practical ordering move a neighborhood spot needs.

Order this too Taquitos + Shrimp Tostadas

The taquitos are three crispy tacos with chicken, lettuce, sour cream, and cheese. The shrimp tostadas add pickled onion, radish, cabbage, cilantro, and habanero dressing.

Do not skip dessert Churros, tres leches, horchata

The churros and pastel de tres leches are both live menu staples, and one Uber Eats reviewer called the tres leches “incredible.” Wash it down with horchata or jamaica.

This place has some fight in it

CityNews Halifax reported that owner Daniela Alonso opened Taquitos in December 2023, then spent early 2026 asking the public for help after rising food costs pushed the business to the edge. The surge of community support after that post is part of the brand now, whether they asked for it or not.

What changed

After the January social post blew up, Alonso said, “It’s been amazing. We have to adjust now to hopefully the new normal that we have more customers.”

What the site should do

Turn that attention into orders, visits, and repeat customers instead of dropping people onto a dead-end ordering setup page.

Go hungry. Bring friends.
