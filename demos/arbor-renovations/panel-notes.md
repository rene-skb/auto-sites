# Panel notes — Arbor Renovations

## Creative direction panel

**Brand brief:** Greater Victoria renovation contractor established in 1999. Owner is the lead carpenter, estimates projects personally, and does most carpentry himself. Broad interior/exterior scope: kitchens, bathrooms, finishing carpentry, doors/windows, decks, stairs, fences, arbors/privacy screens, painting, flooring, drywall, roofing/siding, basement development, plumbing/electrical subcontracting. The current site has useful facts but needs hierarchy, better project proof, and a clearer estimate path.

- **Steve Jobs:** The site should feel like a calm, capable carpenter walking the house with you. Lead with personal responsibility and finished work, not a pile of service links.
- **Paul Graham:** Cut the generic contractor claims. The real differentiator is that the owner estimates personally and does most of the carpentry. Say that plainly.
- **David Ogilvy:** Headline should make the visitor feel safe making the first call: “The owner estimates it. The carpenter builds it.”

## WHY critique

### Steve Jobs — 8.1
- v0 has a real point of view: owner-led renovation, not anonymous contracting.
- The deck/stair imagery gives scale and warmth.
- The service list still feels a little like a directory. Make the page feel more guided.

### Paul Graham — 7.8
- The clearest sentence is owner estimates personally and does most carpentry himself. Move it closer to the first screen.
- Avoid soft phrases like professional, transparent, memorable. The proof is better than the adjective.
- Senior discounts and fair hourly rates are useful, but secondary.

### David Ogilvy — 8.2
- The headline works because it is specific and believable.
- Add a tighter subhead that names Greater Victoria and the project range.
- The proof should mention established in 1999 and A+ BBB without making it a stats strip.

**WHY average:** 8.03

**Next changes:**
1. Move the owner-led proof into the hero support copy.
2. Convert the broad services into guided “inside / outside / finish” paths.
3. Pull established-in-1999 and A+ BBB into prose, not a stats bar.
4. Keep the deck image as the main visual anchor.

## WHAT critique

### Don Norman — 8.1
- The visitor can understand the offer quickly: call for a free estimate or consultation.
- Service categories are easier after grouping, but each group should answer what kind of project fits.
- The contact section needs business hours in plain sight.

### Steve Krug — 8.0
- Navigation is simple and the phone CTA is obvious.
- The page scans well on desktop.
- The quote/review section should use shorter excerpts so people actually read them.

### Jakob Nielsen — 7.9
- Strong readability overall.
- Review attributions need first name plus last initial format to satisfy the house rule; convert formal names carefully from the source.
- Keep body text under three sentences per block.

**WHAT average:** 8.00

**Next changes:**
1. Add concise explanatory text to each service group.
2. Shorten review excerpts and format attributions as compliant first-name + last-initial where possible.
3. Put Monday-Saturday 8:30 am-8:30 pm in the final estimate section.
4. Reduce repeated CTA language.

## HOW critique

### Massimo Vignelli — 8.2
- The editorial grid feels steady and contractor-appropriate.
- The palette is restrained: warm cream, cedar, dark green-black.
- Reduce borders where spacing already separates sections.

### Erik Spiekermann — 8.1
- Newsreader gives the page a residential editorial tone without feeling precious.
- IBM Plex Sans is a solid workhorse for service details.
- Increase line height slightly in dark sections and tighten display line breaks.

### Dieter Rams — 8.2
- The design is useful and not decorative.
- Remove any duplicate service mentions that exist only to reassure.
- Let the project photos carry the proof rather than adding extra badges.

### Emil Kowalski — 8.3
- Motion should be restrained: section reveal, staggered service cards, button active states.
- Do not animate project images on hover.
- Keep content visible without JavaScript.

**HOW average:** 8.20

**Next changes:**
1. Simplify border treatment in the service cards.
2. Increase dark-section line height.
3. Add restrained scroll reveals with noscript fallback.
4. Make final image captions concrete by trade and room/area.
