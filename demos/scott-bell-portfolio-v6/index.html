01 —

Strike

Fintech
Bitcoin Payments
2021–23

Strike needed UX that could move as fast as money does. The Lightning Network is technically remarkable — but entirely invisible to users. Every screen was a translation problem: make instant global payments feel as natural as texting someone $20.

I designed the core payment experience across send, receive, and currency exchange — systems that worked across US, El Salvador, and Argentina, each with completely different monetary contexts.

Outcome: Strike reached $1B+ valuation. The UX I designed is used by millions across 100+ countries, including El Salvador's national Bitcoin rollout.

02 —

Fountain

Consumer
Bitcoin Podcasting
2023

Fountain sat at the intersection of something genuinely new: streaming micropayments woven into the act of listening. Every minute you listen, sats flow to the creator. The design challenge wasn't payments — it was making them feel invisible until they were magical.

I redesigned the core listening experience, discovery system, and social layer — keeping Bitcoin as the empowering undercurrent, not the attention-grabbing feature.

Outcome: Shipped a redesigned app that made value-for-value podcasting feel natural for an audience encountering Bitcoin payments for the first time.

03 —

AIOZ
Network

AI Infrastructure
Web3
2024–25

AIOZ builds AI infrastructure for a decentralized world — where users are both developers building tools and non-technical participants providing compute. Designing across that spectrum required holding contradictory user models simultaneously.

I designed dTransfer (decentralized file delivery), the community platform, and the AI models interface — each requiring a different register of complexity made accessible.

Outcome: Multiple product surfaces shipped at a $1B+ market cap AI/Web3 infrastructure company, bridging technical depth with accessible UX across developer and consumer audiences.

Current direction

Designing the gap
between humans
and agents.

The shift from tools to agents changes everything about how we design. Users aren't just interacting anymore — they're collaborating. The interface isn't a form. The output isn't a file. The relationship is ongoing.

I'm building LoopIn — a browser bookmarklet that captures UI context and routes it directly to Claude Code via MCP server. It's a hands-on exploration of the question consuming me: how do you give an AI agent the right context at the right moment?

This is where my background — translating hidden structure into usable interfaces — meets the hardest UX problems of the next decade.

I'm a product designer with over a decade of making the complex feel simple. My process starts with the hidden structure — the logic underneath the surface — and works outward to something that feels inevitable once you see it.

I've led at $1B+ market cap scale, built design systems from scratch, taught professionally, and mentored teams. I work across research, UX, and visual design — not as separate disciplines, but as one continuous act of making sense.

Right now I'm focused on the most interesting design problem of our moment: how do humans and AI agents work together well? Not AI features. Not chatbot UX. The full texture of collaboration between a person and a system that can think.

I believe good design reveals structure, invites clarity, and brings coherence to complexity. That's as true for a Bitcoin wallet as it is for an agent interface.
