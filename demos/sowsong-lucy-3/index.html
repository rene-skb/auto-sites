Tia Noelle Soby

A former florist who apprenticed with Vancouver Island metalsmiths and opened Sow Song in 2025. She works from a converted trailer studio in Nanaimo and takes consultations in Tofino.

The name comes from a song her Grandpa Soby sang on banjo to the grandkids. Playful and full of love. That energy is in every piece.
