About

Organic dairy from the island. Fruit from the valley.

Organic milk from Vancouver Island farms. Strawberries picked that morning. Rhubarb from a friend's garden. That's the recipe, more or less.

Everything is made in small batches at the shop on Esquimalt Road. When a flavour's done for the season, it's done. That's what makes the next one worth waiting for.
