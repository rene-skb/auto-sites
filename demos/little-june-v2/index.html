Designed with the building, not for it.

Little June sits at Gladstone and Fernwood, across from the Belfry Theatre. The cafe was built in cooperation with the space. The exposed brick, the long window bar, the morning light. None of it's accidental.

Matcha is whisked by hand with a traditional chasen. Sandwiches are stacked on house-baked bread. The playlist is always right. And it's full every day, which tells you more than we could.
