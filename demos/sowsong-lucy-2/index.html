An Ode to Love · In dedication to Grandpa Soby

Named after a song her grandfather played on banjo.

"Sow Song was a tune he'd sing to us grandkids most times we were with him. Playful and full of love. When I make jewelry, I'm always thinking about connection."

Tia Noelle Soby makes jewelry in a converted Travelux trailer on Vancouver Island. She apprenticed with local mentors, learned metalsmithing by hand, and works with one client at a time.
