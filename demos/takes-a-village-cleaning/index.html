About Lana

"No one should be ashamed of mess and grime. There are so many reasons why someone's home might not look the way they want it to."

She Shows You Her Mess First

Most cleaning services sell you a spotless fantasy. Lana starts by showing you her own home — dishes in the sink, laundry on the couch, life happening everywhere. That radical honesty built a community of nearly a million people who finally feel like it's okay to ask for help.

Lana grew up surrounded by artists on Vancouver Island. She carries that same eye for detail into every home, but Takes a Village was never about perfection. It's about showing up without judgment.

When families are struggling — with mental health, grief, a new baby, a hard season — Lana does free cleanings to help them breathe again. Because sometimes the most powerful thing isn't a clean house. It's knowing someone cared enough to come.

The Name

We Used to Share the Load

The saying goes "it takes a village." But most of us don't have one anymore. We live behind closed doors, carrying everything alone, ashamed to ask for help. Lana started this business to be the village — the person who shows up without judgment and helps you breathe again.

Free Cleanings

A Fresh Start for Families Who Need One

Lana does free cleanings for people going through hard times. Mental health crises, postpartum, grief, disability, tough seasons. No application form, no proving you deserve it. Just reach out.

This isn't a marketing angle. It's the reason the business exists. The paid work funds the free work. You support the mission just by being a client.
