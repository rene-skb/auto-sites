## WHY critique

**Steve Jobs — 8.0**
- The hero has a real stance: prepared, accountable local help.
- The collage makes the current official assets feel better without pretending they are documentary proof.
- Push the office/free estimate/guarantee higher so the visitor feels trust early.

**Paul Graham — 7.8**
- The strongest sentences are the plain ones. Keep “licensed and insured,” “since 1998,” and “free estimate.”
- Cut soft words around “craft” unless the source says it.
- The Pasadena service area should be scannable, not buried.

**David Ogilvy — 8.1**
- The headline is believable because it names a contractor pain.
- The CTA pair is direct. “Call” and “Request” beat clever labels.
- Reviews should stay short and attributed, even if that means fewer cards.

**WHY average:** 7.97

**Changes for v1**

2. Move local proof, guarantee, free estimate, and office details above the fold.
3. Strip any unsourced “premium craft” language.
4. Use only attributed review snippets.
