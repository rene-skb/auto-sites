We are a family owned business running in the cities for over 30 years and we hold our standards very high when it comes to tiling.  David Lord is a skilled craftsman in the industry and always aims to provide maximum effort and satisfaction when it comes to clients. At Lord Tile, we understand how sentimental your new home renovation is and we strive to exceed your expectations. That being said, we value your business and happiness.

David Lord, our tiling specialist, is a certified ceramic and mosaic tile specialist with a professional C-54 Tile Contractor CSLB License #983081.
