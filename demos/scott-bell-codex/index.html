About

The layer underneath.

Education BSc Microbiology BA Interaction Design
Location Victoria, BC
Experience 14+ years

I started in microbiology. Four years studying systems you can't see with the naked eye. Then I went back to school for interaction design, because I realized the same skill applies: find the hidden structure, make it visible, make it useful.

Fourteen years later, the pattern holds. At KOHO I found the hidden layer in banking (people hate their bank, so don't make it feel like one). At Strike I found it in Bitcoin (the technology should be invisible). At AIOZ I found it in chaos (8 products need one language).

I'm not the designer who makes things pretty. I'm the one who figures out what the product is actually about, underneath all the features and requirements and stakeholder opinions. Then I make that real.

"Every product has a logic underneath that nobody sees. I find it. Then I turn it into something you can hold in your hand."
