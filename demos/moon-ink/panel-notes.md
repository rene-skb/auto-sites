# Moon Ink — Panel Notes

## WHY critique
**Reviewers:** Steve Jobs, Paul Graham, David Ogilvy

### Steve Jobs — 7.5
- The dragon hero gives the page real presence fast.
- The core idea is there: detail, range, control.
- Cut any line that sounds like commentary on the existing site. Lead with what Moon Ink is.

### Paul Graham — 7.0
- The page gets stronger when it stays literal.
- "The range is the pitch" is useful because it says what matters in plain English.
- Some copy still explains the strategy instead of just showing the thing.

### David Ogilvy — 8.0
- "For people who want detail made permanent" is the right kind of headline, specific enough to feel human.
- The style list is clearer than a generic about paragraph.
- Proof copy should be shorter and more direct.

**Panel average:** 7.5

**Next changes for Lucy:**
1. Strip out meta-commentary and let the work lead.
2. Tighten hero and section copy so the idea lands faster.
3. Keep the page built around detail, range, and calm atmosphere.

## WHAT critique
**Reviewers:** Don Norman, Steve Krug, Jakob Nielsen

### Don Norman — 7.5
- The flow makes sense: hero, styles, work, proof, visit.
- The visit section does a good job of answering practical questions.
- Some paragraphs can lose a sentence without losing meaning.

### Steve Krug — 7.5
- I can tell what this place does in a few seconds.
- Labels work, but proof and visit cards need shorter copy for better scanning.
- The page feels easier once the live-site explanation is reduced.

### Jakob Nielsen — 7.0
- Good restraint on motion and contrast is mostly solid.
- The lack of compliant named reviews is handled honestly, which is better than faking it.
- Small supporting text needed a touch more contrast and some paragraphs were still dense.

**Panel average:** 7.33

**Next changes for Lucy:**
1. Shorten proof and visit paragraphs.
2. Raise contrast on subtle/supporting text.
3. Keep labels and CTA paths very obvious.

## HOW critique
**Reviewers:** Massimo Vignelli, Erik Spiekermann, Dieter Rams, Emil Kowalski

### Massimo Vignelli — 7.5
- The page has a coherent system and the image-top-text-below hero works with the square artwork.
- The section rhythm is mostly clean.
- Keep reducing decorative explanation and let the grid do more of the talking.

### Erik Spiekermann — 7.5
- Instrument Serif + Work Sans is a sensible pairing for this studio, calm without turning precious.
- Body type is readable; smaller labels and captions needed slightly stronger contrast.
- The hierarchy gets better when card copy is shorter.

### Dieter Rams — 7.0
- Honest restraint is the best part here.
- No fake reviews, no map clutter, no image gimmicks. Good.
- Still a little too much explanation in the visit section. Less, but not bare.

### Emil Kowalski — 7.5
- Motion should stay simple: reveal, stagger, button press.
- No image hover animation, which is correct.
- Keep everything on transform and opacity only.

**Panel average:** 7.38

**Next changes for Lucy:**
1. Tighten spacing and copy in the proof + visit blocks.
2. Lift subtle text contrast slightly.
3. Keep motion restrained and applied only to reveals, stagger, and button feedback.
