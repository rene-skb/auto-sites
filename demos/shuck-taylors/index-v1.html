The Story
"Good ideas take time."

Owner Jess Taylor grew up on Vancouver Island, spent time on the East Coast, then worked at Rodney's Oyster House in Vancouver. He started building the vision in 2018. Shuck Taylor's opened in 2023. Five years of planning, then one of the best-reviewed restaurants in BC.

Before opening, Jess took his entire team to Shelton, WA for five days to immerse them in oyster culture. The oyster knowledge was non-negotiable. That level of care is why the seafood here hits different.
