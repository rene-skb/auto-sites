The Maker

"Starting this little heartfilled business has meant more to me than I can say."

Tia Noelle Soby makes jewelry from a converted Travelux trailer on Vancouver Island. She apprenticed with local mentors, learned metalsmithing by hand, and works with one client at a time.

Her background is in floristry — the same care for living, impermanent things carries into everything she makes. Nature flows through her process and into each piece.

Based in Nanaimo  ·  Consultations in Tofino

An Ode to Love

In dedication to Grandpa Soby

"Sow Song was a tune he'd sing to us grandkids most times we were with him. Playful and full of love. When I make jewelry, I'm always thinking about connection."

"I want the pieces I make to hold meaning throughout our lifetime. I like to think of them as tokens of love — some remind us of the people we care about, the way Sow Song reminds me of him."

— Tia Noelle Soby
