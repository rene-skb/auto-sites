Our Story

Started with a roaster and a dream about good mornings

Maya and Jess opened Spiral in 2019 with a simple idea: a neighbourhood spot where the coffee is worth lingering over and the people behind the counter actually know your name.

We roast our own beans in the back room every Tuesday. Our pastries come out of the oven before dawn. The art on the walls changes monthly, always from someone local. And on Friday evenings, the tables get pushed back for live music — no cover, no pretension, just a room full of neighbours.

We're not trying to be everything. We're trying to be your favourite place within walking distance.
