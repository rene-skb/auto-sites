The Cook

One woman. One kitchen. One mission.

Stir It Up is Caribbean food the way it's supposed to taste. The roti gets rolled by hand. The curry simmers all morning. The jerk sits in its marinade until it's ready. Nothing comes from a box. Straight from Saint Lucia to this alley off Yates.
