The Shop

Same Spot. Same Chair.
Every Time.

Rahim and his team cut hair in Collingwood seven days a week. No booking apps, no waitlists. You walk in, you sit down, you walk out looking right.

Regulars don't explain what they want. Families bring their kids. Guys come in on lunch. This is the neighbourhood chair — the one that's always there when you need it.
