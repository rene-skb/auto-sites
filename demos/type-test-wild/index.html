Our Story
OLD-WORLD
CRAFT

Established in 1983, Yoka's Coffee is a ma and pa roaster in the North Park neighbourhood of Victoria, British Columbia. We've always had a simple objective: fresh-roasted, premium coffee at affordable prices.

Originally operating in Vancouver, we relocated to Victoria in 2010. Stop by during the week and you'll likely find us roasting — all our beans are roasted in the shop on our mid-1930s Royal roaster. We're proud to have one of the widest selections of medium and dark-roasted, certified organic and fair traded coffees in the city.

We also sell green beans for home roasters, loose tea, local honey, and chocolate.
