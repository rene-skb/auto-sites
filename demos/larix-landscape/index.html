The real brief

A good yard here has to manage water first.

Victoria projects ask for two things at once: outdoor space you want to use, and a site plan that deals with slope, pooling, soil, patios, walls, and foundation water.

Drainage

Pooling, soggy lawns, and perimeter drains.

Larix installs, repairs, cleans, and inspects drainage systems, including camera scope inspections and foundation waterproofing work.

Construction

Walls, patios, decks, fences, and pergolas.

One team can plan the garden and build the hard pieces around it: retaining walls, paving, concrete, custom woodwork, stairs, and outdoor rooms.

Planting

Garden beds that fit the site.
