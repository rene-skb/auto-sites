# Bakerbots Baking — Brand Research Brief

- **Business:** Bakerbots Baking
- **City:** Toronto, ON
- **Category:** Bakery / dessert shop / ice cream sandwiches
- **Address:** 1242 Bloor St W, Toronto
- **Phone:** 416-901-3500
- **Hours:** Tue-Thu 3PM-10PM | Fri 3PM-11PM | Sat 11AM-11PM | Sun 11AM-10PM | Mon closed
- **Social:** @bakerbots_baking
- **Website status:** Live but fragmented. Root page mostly punts to an older ice cream page and Square ordering flow.

## Brand read
- **Colors:** cream, butter yellow, chocolate brown, warm red
- **Vibe:** playful neighbourhood dessert counter, late-hours bakery energy, not precious
- **Difference:** people know them for the ice cream sandwiches, but editorial coverage keeps pointing at the deeper bench: pies, cookies, cakes, puffs, pastry case variety
- **Typography direction:** high-character serif up top, warm utility sans underneath
