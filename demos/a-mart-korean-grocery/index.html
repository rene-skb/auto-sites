About

"You walk in for gochujang and leave with a bag full of things you didn't know you wanted."

A Mart sits on Yates Street in the middle of downtown Victoria. The space is small. The shelves are not. From fresh perilla leaves and house-made kimchi to frozen mandu and every sauce in the Korean kitchen, this is what happens when someone stocks a store like they're cooking for their own family.

The staff know every product on the shelf and they'll tell you what to do with it. First-timer making japchae? They'll walk you through it.

The Name

Every Aisle Is a Shortcut Home

For Victoria's Korean community, A Mart is the closest thing to walking through a neighbourhood grocery in Seoul. For everyone else, it's where you discover that Korean cooking starts with better ingredients.

What We Carry

Korean Essentials, All in One Place

01

The Sauce Wall

Gochujang, doenjang, ssamjang, sesame oil, fish sauce, Korean soy sauce, rice vinegar. The foundation of every Korean dish.

02

Fresh & Frozen

House kimchi, tteok (rice cakes), frozen mandu (dumplings), fresh perilla, enoki mushrooms, and seasonal Korean produce.

03

The Snack Aisle

Seaweed snacks, honey butter chips, shrimp crackers, Shin Ramyeon, barley tea, soju, makgeolli, and Yakult.

04

Rice & Noodles

Premium Korean short-grain rice, japchae glass noodles, naengmyeon, and every instant noodle variety you can't find at Save-On.
