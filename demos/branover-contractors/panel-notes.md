# Panel notes — Branover Contractors Inc

## WHY critique

**Steve Jobs — 8.1**
- The page has a real center: inspection-ready electrical work. That feels specific to this contractor.
- The hero should make license and phone proof visible without turning into a checklist.

**Paul Graham — 7.9**
- The plain words work. “Panel upgrades, EV chargers, code corrections” beats abstract trust language.
- Cut anything that sounds like an audit of the old site. Customers just need the facts.

**David Ogilvy — 8.2**
- The headline sells a concrete outcome: work that can pass inspection.
- Keep the phone number in the CTA. It’s the conversion path.

**Panel average:** 8.07

**Next changes**
1. Move C10 license proof into the hero badge.
2. Keep the headline under 5 words.
3. Bring the phone number into every major CTA.

## WHAT critique

**Don Norman — 8.2**
- The quick info strip answers the practical questions early.
- Service categories are understandable and mapped to real customer needs.

**Steve Krug — 8.1**
- Visitors know what to do: call.
- Keep the process section short. People hiring electricians are not reading a manifesto.

**Jakob Nielsen — 8.0**
- Strong scannability through labels, short paragraphs, and high contrast.
- Mobile needs the same clear service hierarchy and a working menu from the first build.

**Panel average:** 8.10

**Next changes**
1. Keep the quick info strip directly under the hero.
2. Limit service copy to 2 sentences per card.
3. Preserve one primary action: call Branover.

## HOW critique

**Massimo Vignelli — 8.2**
- The grid has authority. The warm paper and utility blue give the page discipline.
- Reduce red to signal moments only.

**Erik Spiekermann — 8.1**
- Source Serif adds weight without nostalgia; Space Grotesk works for labels and technical rhythm.
- Watch long line lengths in the hero and proof quote.

**Dieter Rams — 8.2**
- The page has enough. Do not add another proof module or stats bar.
- Keep the generated images secondary and clearly non-representational.

**Emil Kowalski — 8.3**
- Motion should be standard scroll reveal plus button feedback.
- No image hover scale. Exact transition properties only.

**Panel average:** 8.20

**Next changes**
1. Use motion only for reveal and feedback.
2. Keep all review cards on the same treatment.
3. Final check for image hover, source-language leakage, and legal claim specificity.
