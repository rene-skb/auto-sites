Our Story

Started with a collection
that outgrew the apartment

Deep Cuts Vinyl was founded in 2009 by Ray and Sofia Muñoz, two music heads who realized their East Austin apartment couldn't hold one more crate. So they rented a storefront on East 6th, filled it with records, and opened the doors.

Sixteen years later, the collection has grown to over 30,000 records. The storefront got bigger. The couple got married in the shop (between the jazz and soul sections, naturally). And the regulars became family.

We buy collections, trade with other shops across Texas, and press limited runs with local labels. Every record in the store has been hand-picked. If it's in the bin, someone here believes in it.
