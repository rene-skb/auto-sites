The Place

Ethan Choi and Zoey Jung opened URL Coffee in March 2021.

The name is a tech pun — perfectly tuned to Seattle's First Hill neighborhood.

Mid-century modern with a note of punkish whimsy. The stools are made from skateboard decks. The light is always good.
