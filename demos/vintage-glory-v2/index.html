The Shop

The owner knows every piece by name

Vintage Glory is one person's collection, opened to the public. The shop sits at Keenleyside and Thomas in Elmwood Place, packed floor to ceiling with 20th century clothing, accessories, and jewelry.

Reviewers call it "a real vintage shop, not reproductions." The owner curates everything personally and has a story for most of it. Walk-ins Wednesday through Sunday. Monday and Tuesday are by appointment only.
