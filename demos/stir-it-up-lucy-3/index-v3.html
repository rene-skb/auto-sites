What to Order

Jerk chicken. Oxtail. Roti wraps. Patties.

Jerk Chicken

Charcoal-kissed, slow-marinated in scotch bonnet and allspice. Served over rice and peas with a fried plantain on the side.

House Favourite

Oxtail

Braised low and slow until the meat falls off the bone. Rich brown gravy, butter beans, and thyme. Get there early — it goes fast.

Roti Wraps

Handmade dhalpuri roti, rolled fresh and stuffed with curried chicken, potato, or vegetables. The pepper sauce is made in-house.

Jamaican Patties

Flaky pastry shells filled with seasoned beef or chicken. Turmeric-yellow crust, spiced filling. Good on their own, better with a Grace Soda.

St. Lucian Soul Food

St. Lucian cooking. Made fresh in Victoria.

The food comes from St. Lucia — a specific island with specific flavors. Scotch bonnet heat. Allspice from the Pitons. Curried roti with dhalpuri dough, not a shortcut substitute. Pepper sauce that actually bites back.

Everything here is made from scratch, the day it's served. That's why the hours are what they are.

Fresh Ingredients

Fresh callaloo. Not from a supplier.

Callaloo is a leafy green used across the Caribbean — in soups, stews, and sides. At Stir It Up it shows up in multiple dishes, and it's sourced directly, not ordered from a distributor.

Small choices like that add up to food that tastes like it was made by someone who cares.
