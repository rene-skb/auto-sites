The Story

Ice cream that follows the calendar

Kid Sister is Christen's shop on Esquimalt Road. She makes everything in small batches with ingredients from local farms. When the strawberries arrive, there's roasted strawberry. When the lavender blooms, there's lavender honey.

The menu is different every week. That's the whole idea.

Beyond Scoops

Popsicles with pressed flowers. Dorayaki sandwiches. Kombucha floats.

The menu goes wherever Christen's curiosity takes it. Last spring it was cherry blossom popsicles with edible pansies. Before that, dorayaki filled with black sesame ice cream.

Nothing comes from a mix. Nothing stays on the menu forever.
