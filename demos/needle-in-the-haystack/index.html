About Lilia

Thirteen Years in Uniform. Then She Chose This.

"No, that isn't right. I don't like that."

— Lilia, mid-fitting

Lilia Chtcherbakova spent 13 years as a police officer in her native Latvia — not because she loved it, but because the pay was better than working as a seamstress. When she moved to Victoria, she finally chose the craft she was trained for.

Now she runs The Needle in the Haystack on Hillside Avenue, a one-woman European tailoring shop where the standard is exacting and the turnaround is fast. Customers who've been coming for years say the same thing: Lilia doesn't let anything leave her shop unless it's right.

The Name

The One You Stop Looking After

A needle in a haystack — something rare and hard to find. Most people try two or three tailors before they find the one who actually listens, actually adjusts, actually cares. Lilia built the shop where the search ends.

Services

What We Do

01

Clothing Alterations

Hemming, taking in, letting out, shortening sleeves, tapering — the everyday adjustments that make clothes actually fit. Most jobs done in two days. Starting from $15.

02

Bridal & Formal Wear

Wedding dress alterations, bridesmaid fittings, and formal wear adjustments. Lilia treats every gown like a once-in-a-lifetime piece, because it is.

03

Dressmaking

Custom dressmaking from scratch. Bring your vision, your fabric, or just an idea — Lilia will figure out the rest.

04

European Tailoring

Structured tailoring in the European tradition — jackets, trousers, coats. Precise fitting, clean lines, proper construction.
