# Scott Bell Sonnet Proof — Rationale

## Slug
- `scott-bell-sonnet-proof`

## Intent
Rebuild Scott Bell’s portfolio demo from scratch with a visibly higher craft bar than the previous proof. Keep the strongest factual content and chapter structure, but replace the SaaS-y card/template feel with a warmer editorial direction led by typography, space, and restrained motion.

## Creative direction
- **Mood:** dark editorial opening, warm paper chapters, restrained amber accent
- **Typography:** Cormorant Garamond for display and chapter headlines, Space Grotesk for body and UI
- **Structure:** Current Work → Selected Work → About → Contact
- **Principle:** let the work carry the proof, and weave metrics into sentences instead of lifting them into stats bars

## What I kept
### From the previous portfolio proof
- The strongest factual inventory and proof points for Strike, AIOZ, Fountain, KOHO, and Nakamoto Design Co.
- The chapter pacing: recent self-built work first, career proof second, about and contact last
- The hero opener: `24 products in 14 years. The last four, I built myself.`

### From the stronger reference builds
- `scott-bell-opus` → proof-led writing, concrete credibility, stronger Strike/AIOZ/Fountain detail
- `scott-bell-portfolio-v8` → chapter clarity and the `Scientist brain. Design hands.` framing
- `scott-bell-v2` → breadth framing and the current-work-first positioning

## What changed
- Removed the generic portfolio/template energy from the old build: no purple accent, no rounded SaaS cards, no stats bars, no Inter-first look
- Rebuilt the current work section so Nakamoto Design Co. feels like a serious AI-native studio proof, not a side project block
- Switched the recent self-built projects from text-only list treatment into visual editorial panels with real previews
- Strengthened the emotional throughline with the amber quote ribbon: `I’m most useful early...`
- Tightened the selected work section so each case study explains not just what happened, but what made the work hard
- Kept the About section factual and portfolio-relevant instead of drifting into lifestyle bio territory

## Fact inventory used in copy
- `24 products in 14 years` → from prior Scott portfolio builds
- Strike: `1.5M+ users`, `65+ countries`, `450+ screens`, `5 months` → from `scott-bell-opus` / prior proof rationale
- AIOZ: `8 products`, `market cap from $13M to $1.38B`, `~100× growth` → from `scott-bell-opus` / prior proof rationale
- Fountain: `full rebuild in under five months`, `Apple featured twice`, semantic design system framing → from `scott-bell-opus` / prior proof rationale
- KOHO: `employee #1`, `0 to 100K+ users`, `$800M+ valuation` → from `scott-bell-opus` / prior proof rationale
- Nakamoto: `80+ demos`, pipeline framing → from the previous proof rationale and existing assets/context

## Why the final version is stronger
- The page reads like a designed portfolio, not a startup landing page template
- Typography and spacing do most of the work, which gives the proof more confidence
- The dark-to-warm chapter flow makes the page feel authored instead of assembled
- Metrics stay in the copy where they feel earned
- The latest work now feels directly relevant to Scott’s AI-native positioning, while the legacy work still carries credibility at scale
