Our Story

More Than a Bakery

Fantan Alley

Built by community, for community

Friends & Family Bake started with a baker's passion and a first batch sold through a friend's network. Word spread. Friendships grew. What started small became a beloved Chinatown fixture, one tray of ensaymada at a time.

We're a VIHA-approved, locally owned bakeshop specializing in authentic Filipino breads and pastries alongside Western baked goods. Every recipe is made with care, rooted in tradition, and baked fresh.

Giving back: Part of our proceeds supports the Zoe Children's Home Foundation, providing school supplies and support for children in the Philippines. When you buy from us, you're part of that mission.
