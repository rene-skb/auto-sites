The Bookshops Keep Disappearing. We're Still Here.

When everything moved online, Victoria's used bookstores started closing. One by one. Bastion Books is what happens when someone decides to hold the line — in an alley, in a square that's been the heart of this city since 1863.

We stock vintage and contemporary titles across every genre. If you can't find what you need, tell us — we'll track it down. There's no algorithm here. Just spines, covers, and the thrill of finding something you didn't know you needed.

Every Book Here Had a Life Before You.

Dog-eared pages, pencil notes in margins, a receipt from 1987 used as a bookmark. Second-hand books carry their history with them. We think that makes them better, not worse.
