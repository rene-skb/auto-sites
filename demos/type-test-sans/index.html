About

Hide Out.
Seek Friends.

We built Hide + Seek on a simple idea: great coffee shouldn't feel exclusive. It should feel like your neighbourhood.

Our beans come from local Victoria roasters. Our pastries from Empire Donuts and Crust Bakery. Our mugs from local artists. Everything here supports someone nearby.

Three locations, three neighbourhoods, same vibe. Whether you're hiding out with a book or seeking friends over cortados, there's always a seat.

"Cool music, excellent coffee, made with flair, and run by friendly folks with an interest in you enjoying your drink."
