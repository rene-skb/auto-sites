Since 1989

Vancouver's first desserts-only restaurant.

Alanna and Nadine started True Confections after deciding Vancouver needed a place built around dessert. They spent 2 years testing family recipes in home kitchens before opening in February 1989.

The mission: serve a huge made-from-scratch dessert selection with fresh ingredients. Cakes are still baked daily and both rooms still stay open late.
