More than just a grooming shop

Barry and Busters is a full-service pet grooming shop serving the Greater Victoria area. We work with dogs and cats of all sizes and breeds, giving each one the individual attention they deserve.

We believe grooming should be a positive experience for your pet — not a stressful one. That's why we take our time, use gentle techniques, and make sure every animal leaves happy and looking great.

With two convenient locations in Esquimalt and Sidney, quality pet grooming is always close to home.
