The Story

Everyone Saw Empty Storefronts. They Saw an Opening.

"We're on the hook for nearly $10,000 a month in downtown rent. But look at this block — it's happening."

Brady Oneill, 21 — Victoria News

Brady was 21. Gideon was 20. Daniel was 19. While their friends started university, they signed a lease on Johnson Street. The 600 block was already becoming something — Second Degree Vintage, Lonely Roads, Goodnews Skateshop — and they wanted in.

Every piece in the shop is sourced by hand. They know the difference between vintage and old. Between rare and overpriced. Between a find and a reject. That eye is the whole business.

The Name

The Key Piece Changes Everything

One jacket. One find you didn't know you needed until you tried it on. That's the key piece — the one that makes the whole fit click. They hunt for those pieces every single day so you can walk in and find yours.
