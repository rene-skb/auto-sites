The Kitchen

The Kitchen That Makes Europeans Nostalgic.

"As a European, the pastries make me feel like I'm back home."

When someone from the continent says your croissants taste like home, you've passed the only test that matters. La Roux Patisserie is a chandelier-lit French pastry shop in Victoria's Chinatown — steps from Fan Tan Alley, a world away from everything else.

Everything is handcrafted and made fresh daily. Flaky butter croissants. Delicate macarons. Paris-Brest with real praline cream. And celebration cakes that get photographed before they're cut.
