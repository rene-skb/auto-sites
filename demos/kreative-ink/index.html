What We Do

Custom Tattoo Services

Every piece starts with a conversation. We work with you to design something that's uniquely yours.

Black & Grey Realism

Our signature style. Photorealistic portraits, nature, and detailed compositions in stunning greyscale. Nick's specialty and the work that's won awards.

Full Color

Bold, vibrant color work that pops. From neo-traditional to color realism, we bring your vision to life with a rich, lasting palette.

Custom Design

Bring us your idea, reference, or just a feeling. We'll sketch, refine, and create a one-of-a-kind design built specifically for your body and style.

Cover-Ups & Reworks

Old ink that needs a new life? We specialize in creative cover-ups and reworks that transform what's there into something you'll love.

Large Scale Work

Sleeves, back pieces, chest panels. Multi-session projects planned and executed with precision so every piece flows as one cohesive design.

Consultations

Free initial consultations to discuss your idea, placement, sizing, and pricing. No pressure, just an honest conversation about your tattoo.

Not a Street Shop. A Studio.

Kreative Ink is a private studio in Vic West. Clean, comfortable, and built for the kind of focused work that wins awards. No walk-ins, no rush jobs. Just carefully crafted tattoos by artists who treat every piece like it matters — because it does.

How It Works

Getting a tattoo at Kreative Ink is straightforward. Here's what to expect.

01

Consultation

Call or text us with your idea. We'll chat about design, placement, sizing, and give you an honest quote. Consultations are always free.

02

Design

Your artist creates a custom design based on your conversation. You'll see it before your appointment and we refine until it's exactly right.

03

Session

Come to the studio, get comfortable, and we get to work. Clean, private environment. Larger pieces may be planned across multiple sessions.

04

Aftercare

We walk you through proper aftercare before you leave. Your tattoo is an investment — we make sure it heals perfectly.

Pricing varies by size, detail, and session time. Small pieces start around $150+. Larger custom work is quoted per project. Call for an estimate — no obligation.
