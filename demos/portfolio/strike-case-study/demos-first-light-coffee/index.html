Our Story

Two people, one obsession

Maren and Joel Anderssen started First Light in 2018 with a secondhand drum roaster and a conviction that Vancouver deserved better coffee. Not fancier. Better. Beans sourced directly from farmers they know by name, roasted in batches small enough to taste-check every one.

The name comes from those quiet early mornings in the roastery, when the first light hits Main Street and the first batch is almost ready. That's when the work feels most honest.

How We Roast

01
Source Directly
We buy green beans from farms we've visited, paying above fair-trade prices for exceptional lots.
02
Roast Small
12kg batches on our Probat. Every roast is profiled, tasted, and approved before it goes out.
03
Serve Fresh
Beans rest 7-14 days post-roast. We date every bag and never sell past peak.
