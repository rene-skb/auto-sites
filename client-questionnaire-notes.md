# Client Questionnaire — Quick Notes

*Part of the auto-sites pipeline. Sent after the demo, before the final site.*
*Draft the actual form later (Typeform, Google Form, or custom page).*

## Purpose
Turn the demo into a conversation. Owner sees the site, fills out corrections/preferences, we do a tightening pass. They feel ownership, we get accuracy.

## What to Ask

### Basics
- Business name (exactly as you want it displayed)
- Current location(s) and address(es)
- Current hours (or "check Instagram for hours" if variable)
- Phone number and/or email for the site
- Social media links (Instagram, Facebook, etc.)

### Accuracy Check
- Anything on the demo that's incorrect?
- Any services/products listed that you don't actually offer?
- Any services/products we missed?
- How many locations do you have currently?

### Brand & Visual
- Do you have a logo you'd like to use?
- What are your brand colors? (or: "we'll pull from your signage")
- Any photos you'd like on the site? (upload option)
- Any photos on the demo you'd like removed?

### Voice & Copy
- Is there a tagline or phrase you use for your business?
- Any quotes or reviews you'd especially like featured?
- Anything about your story you'd want visitors to know?
- How would you describe your business in one sentence?

### Optional
- Anything else you'd want on the site?
- Any sites you like the look of? (inspiration)

## Notes
- Keep it SHORT. 10-15 questions max. Business owners are busy.
- Frame it as "help us get this perfect for you" not "fill out this form"
- The demo does the heavy lifting — the form just catches the gaps
- Filling out the form = psychological commitment toward paying
