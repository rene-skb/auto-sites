- No generic address link is used. The final page links only to the exact Google listing URL surfaced by Miller’s homepage.
- No fake storefronts, interiors, building exteriors, generated images, or stock imagery were added.
- Before photos and construction-process images were not used as standalone proof in the final page.
- Full customer names from the source were shortened to first name + last initial in visible quote cards to match Auto-Sites review-card standards.
