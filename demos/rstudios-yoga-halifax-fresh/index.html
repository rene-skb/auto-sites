First Time?

What to expect.

Hot yoga or hot Pilates?

Hot yoga flows in the heated room. Hot Pilates is reformer-based work in the heat. Both are challenging. Both earn the sauna after.

Do I need to be fit to start?

No. The studio is membership-based and welcomes all levels. Call ahead and they'll point you toward the right class.

What's the membership?

RSTUDIOS runs on membership. Call +1 902-912-0207 to ask about membership options and drop-in rates.

Is the sauna included?

The sauna is on-site at both RINSE and RIO. Ask about access when you book.
