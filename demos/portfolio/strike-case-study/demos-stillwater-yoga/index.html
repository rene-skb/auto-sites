Our Classes

Something for every body

Whether you're stepping onto the mat for the first time or deepening a lifelong practice, we have a class that fits.

Vinyasa Flow

60 min · All Levels

Breath-linked movement that builds heat and focus. Our most popular class, with modifications for every level.

Slow Flow

75 min · Beginner Friendly

A gentler pace with longer holds. Perfect for beginners or anyone craving a more meditative practice.

Yin Yoga

75 min · All Levels

Deep, passive stretches held for 3-5 minutes. Targets connective tissue and cultivates stillness.

Power Yoga

60 min · Intermediate

A vigorous, fitness-based approach to vinyasa. Build strength, flexibility, and endurance.

Restorative

90 min · All Levels

Supported by props, you'll melt into deep relaxation. The antidote to a busy life.

Prenatal Yoga

60 min · Prenatal

Safe, nurturing movement designed for every trimester. Breath work, gentle stretching, and community.

Our Story

A studio that feels like home

Stillwater Yoga was born in 2018 from a simple idea: yoga should feel welcoming, not intimidating. Founder Maya Chen spent years practicing in studios that felt exclusive or performative. She wanted something different.

Tucked into the Hawthorne neighborhood, our 2,400 sq ft studio is a warm, light-filled space with bamboo floors, natural light, and room for 25 students. We believe yoga is for everyone — regardless of flexibility, experience, or what you're wearing.

The name "Stillwater" comes from the practice of finding calm within movement. On the mat, in life. That's what we're here for.
