In Dedication to Grandpa Soby

Where the name comes from

"Sow Song was a tune he'd sing to us grandkids most times we were with him. Playful and full of love."

Tia Noelle Soby grew up watching her grandfather play banjo in the garden. When she started her jewelry brand in 2025, she chose the song he sang to the grandkids — because the pieces she makes are meant to carry the same kind of meaning he did.

"When I make jewelry, I'm always thinking about connection. I want the pieces I make to hold meaning throughout our lifetime."

— Tia Noelle, Sow Song

The Maker

Tia Noelle Soby

Tia is based in Nanaimo and trained with local metalsmithing mentors before opening her studio in a converted Travelux trailer. She works with warm yellow gold, salt-and-pepper diamonds, and organic shapes — bezel settings, hand-carved signets, custom memorial pieces.

She does consultations in Tofino. Every piece starts with a conversation — what you're looking for, whether you have heirloom materials to incorporate, what the occasion means to you.

How It Works

A conversation, then a ring.

Start with an email. Tell Tia what you're imagining — the occasion, any stones or metals in mind, whether you have heirloom pieces to work with. She'll write back.

From there, she designs something specific to you. You talk through it, adjust if needed, and settle on something that feels right before she starts making it.

Tia works with engagement rings, wedding bands, memorial pieces, and heirloom remakes. She's based in Nanaimo and does consultations in Tofino.

Custom pieces take time — budget 8–12 weeks for most commissions. Worth asking about timeline early.
