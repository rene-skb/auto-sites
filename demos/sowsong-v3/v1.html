The Name

"Sow Song"

Some things just stay with us. People, songs, scents, memories — my Grandfather was one of those things for me.

My Grandpa Soby was a musical man. Most of my memories of him are of him in his garden or playing the banjo. He was such a warm, social person, and being around him always made things feel a little more sparkly.

Sow Song was a tune he'd sing to us grandkids most times we were with him. Playful and full of love.

When I make jewelry, I'm always thinking about connection. I want the pieces I make to hold meaning throughout our lifetime. I like to think of them as tokens of love — some remind us of the people we care about, the way Sow Song reminds me of him.

With Love Always,
Tia

The Maker

Tia Noelle

After years as a florist, Tia was drawn to the timelessness of jewellery and apprenticed with local mentors to master the art of metalsmithing.

Today, she works from a converted Travelux trailer studio — an intimate, soulful space that reflects her Western roots and honours her late grandfather, the inspiration behind the name.

Rooted on the coast, Sow Song sources continuous inspiration being among the trees and bodies of water. The wisdom of nature flows throughout her process.
