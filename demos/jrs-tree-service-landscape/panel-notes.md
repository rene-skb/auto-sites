# Panel notes — JR's Tree Service and Landscape

## WHY critique

### Steve Jobs — 8.0
- The dark oak-canopy hero feels like a serious Pasadena contractor, not a lawn-care template.
- The central idea is close: certified care for old, valuable trees.
- Tighten the headline so urgent service buyers hear “Pasadena tree care” faster.

### Paul Graham — 7.8
- The site is specific: Pasadena, ISA arborist, licensed/bonded/insured, real services.
- Some copy talks about source discipline instead of the customer problem.
- Cut internal explanations and keep the plain facts.

### David Ogilvy — 8.1
- “Certified tree care” is a sellable promise because it separates JR from chainsaw-only competitors.
- Phone-first CTA is right.
- Replace any vague secondary CTA with direct service navigation.

**WHY average:** 7.97

**Next changes**
1. Recut the hero headline from “foothill properties” to clearer Pasadena-property language.
2. Remove internal/source-facing credibility copy.
3. Change “See the work” to “View services.”
4. Keep ISA certification and licensed/bonded/insured proof near the top.

## WHAT critique

### Don Norman — 8.1
- The page order is easy to understand: proof, services, arborist rationale, reviews, contact.
- The map embed risks looking broken in screenshot/headless contexts.
- Service area and hours should be explicit in the contact chapter.

### Steve Krug — 8.2
- Phone number is visible and repeated.
- Service cards scan well.
- Remove phrases like “these are the services JR's lists” because visitors don't care how the copy was sourced.

### Jakob Nielsen — 7.8
- Contrast and body sizing are solid.
- Mobile menu exists, and cards collapse cleanly.
- Google Maps iframe can fail in headless or privacy-restricted browsers; provide a clear Maps link.

**WHAT average:** 8.03

**Next changes**
1. Replace the fragile map embed with a service-area card and Google Maps listing CTA.
2. Remove public copy that references the source-gathering process.
3. Keep hours, phone, and areas in the contact section.
4. Preserve first-name/last-initial review attributions.

## HOW critique

### Massimo Vignelli — 8.0
- The grid is coherent and section chapters have clear roles.
- The hero type is almost too large for the category.
- Service cards benefit from the strict 4-column rhythm.

### Erik Spiekermann — 8.2
- Newsreader gives the site mature authority without becoming fancy.
- Source Sans 3 is a good utility counterweight.
- Reduce hero scale slightly to avoid editorial excess.

### Dieter Rams — 7.9
- The design is useful and restrained.
- Remove the map iframe rather than decorating around a broken rectangle.
- No stats bar, no image hover tricks, no fake storefronts.

### Emil Kowalski — 8.1
- Existing scroll reveal pattern is enough.
- Keep stagger on service cards and section entrances only.
- Button active scale is present; reduced-motion support is present.

**HOW average:** 8.05

**Next changes**
1. Reduce hero display size slightly.
2. Add only subtle service-card depth, no decorative motion.
3. Preserve restrained reveal/stagger/button motion.
4. Keep all images unique and non-zooming.
