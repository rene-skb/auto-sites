New Here?

What to expect your first class.

01
Wear something you'd sweat in

Form-fitting works best — loose fabric holds heat in a way that gets uncomfortable. Bring a water bottle. We have mats, or bring your own.

02
The room is genuinely hot

That's the point. If you need to take a knee, take a knee. Instructors are watching and will help you pace yourself. Nobody's judging.

03
Call ahead to confirm the schedule

Classes vary by day. A quick call to +1 902-912-0207 confirms timing, tells you about drop-in rates, and gets you set up for your first session.
