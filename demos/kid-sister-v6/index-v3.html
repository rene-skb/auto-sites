Our Process

From the ground to the scoop

Most ice cream shops buy a pre-made base and add flavour. We make ours from scratch, which is rare in Canada. It takes longer, but you can taste the difference.

01

We make our own ice cream base with organic dairy from Vancouver Island farms.

02

We pick our own fruit, forage for wild ingredients, and source from local growers when the season allows.

03

Everything is churned in small batches at the shop. When a flavour's gone, it's gone until next year.

About

A tiny shop, a kei truck, and whatever's ripe

Kid Sister started with a freezer and a farmers market table. Now there's a shop on Esquimalt Road and a kei truck that shows up at festivals and block parties around Victoria.

Rhubarb in spring. Strawberries in summer. Plums in fall. The ice cream is seasonal because the ingredients are. 5.0 stars on Google.

The fruit comes from local farms, the dairy from island cows, the flowers from nearby gardens. We work with growers and makers who care about the same things we do.

Growing more than you can eat?

If you've got surplus fruit, berries, or herbs, we'd love to hear from you. Drop us a line.
