# Panel Notes — PowerRoute Electric & Plumbing

## WHY critique

### Steve Jobs — 8.1
- The hero has a real promise: an electrician who answers when the problem is live.
- Push the 24/7 and warranty proof closer to the first screen.
- Avoid the family/neighbors language from the current site. It sounds soft for an emergency trade.

### Paul Graham — 7.8
- The page is clearer when it talks about concrete jobs: panel, charger, repair, lighting.
- Cut any sentence that explains the redesign. Visitors don't care about the audit.
- Don't use reviews unless the names are clean.

### David Ogilvy — 8.2
- The headline has tension and a reason to keep reading.
- The phone number should be visible immediately and again at the close.
- Warranty and licensing are selling facts. Keep them near the CTA.

**WHY average:** 8.03

**Concrete next changes**
1. Surface 24/7 and 2-year warranty in the hero card.
2. Replace broad trust copy with licensed/bonded/insured, since 2014, and service-area proof.
3. Keep review cards out unless names are verified.
4. Make the phone CTA the primary action.

## WHAT critique

### Don Norman — 8.0
- The service categories are understandable and match homeowner intent.
- The process needs concrete actions, not empty 1-2-3 labels.
- Avoid a generic address/map link because it creates a wrong affordance.

### Steve Krug — 8.1
- A visitor can tell what to do: call or request a quote.
- The service grid scans well, but the page needs a short warning-sign section for urgency.
- The phone number should not appear so many times that it feels desperate.

### Jakob Nielsen — 7.8
- Mobile needs single-column cards and full-width CTA buttons.
- Dark-section contrast is acceptable if body copy stays bright enough.
- No prices are better than invented price anchors.

**WHAT average:** 7.97

**Concrete next changes**
1. Turn the process into three real steps.
2. Add a short warning-sign list.
3. Keep contact actions focused: phone and official quote form.
4. Verify mobile card wrapping and CTA width.

## HOW critique

### Massimo Vignelli — 8.0
- The system is strongest when dark command-center sections alternate with cream practical sections.
- The proof cards should share one treatment.
- Remove decorative elements that don't carry information.

### Erik Spiekermann — 8.2
- Space Grotesk is doing useful display work. IBM Plex Sans keeps the trade copy readable.
- Tighten large headline letterspacing carefully on mobile.
- Keep review/proof body copy out of display type.

### Dieter Rams — 7.9
- The page earns most sections, but any repeated proof should be cut.
- The EV charger image is useful because it introduces a different job type.
- No stats bar. The facts are stronger in sentences and small cards.

### Emil Kowalski — 8.1
- Use standard scroll reveal only. Let the cards arrive quietly.
- Button press feedback is enough interaction.
- No hover zoom on images.

**HOW average:** 8.05

**Concrete next changes**
1. Use one proof-card treatment everywhere.
2. Add restrained scroll reveal and button active states.
3. Keep image motion static.
4. Run grep checks for source language, em dashes, stats bars, and image hover rules.
