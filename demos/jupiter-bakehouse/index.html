What to get

Start with the bread, then wreck your plan with pastry.

The draw here is the split personality. Hardy sourdough that disappears fast, then pastry that still feels light on the way out. blogTO singled out the sesame sourdough, Ontario sourdough, olive and polenta fougasse, blueberry danish, tiramisu danish, buckwheat financier, and smoked-cheese pretzel for a reason.

Three easy calls.

Sesame or Ontario sourdough Start with the bread that made the farmers' market following translate into a real storefront.
Tiramisu or blueberry danish Jupiter leans French with laminated pastry, not just another loaf counter with a croissant on the side.
Smoked-cheese pretzel The savory move, if you don't want to leave holding only sugar and butter.

Why this place matters

A farmers' market bakery that got its own corner.

Jupiter started in 2023 as a farmers' market operation before taking over the old Glory Hole Doughnuts space in late 2025. Press coverage keeps coming back to the same thing: Forster trained seriously, uses flour from Howick Community Farms, and built the shop around simple bread that tastes like real grain instead of stabilizers and shortcuts.

From the bakehouse

Good bread first. The rest follows.

Jupiter's own materials are pretty restrained. That's part of the appeal. No giant brand speech, just bread, pastry, espresso, and a clear schedule.

The useful detail is underneath that restraint: Blackbird Baking and Petite Thuet in the background, Ontario ingredients, and long fermentation at the center of the menu.

If you want the newest pastry run or the loaf people were lining up for that morning, Instagram is still the fastest read on what's happening day to day.

Visit

Go early if you care about bread.

Jupiter has a live site now, but the useful behavior still feels like a neighbourhood bakery: check Instagram, show up during the Wednesday to Sunday window, and don't assume the best loaf will still be there late.
