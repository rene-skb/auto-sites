The Story

"Authentic Food Is Made as Original"

Ali Syahbana — everyone calls him Bana — cooks the recipes he grew up with in West Sumatra. Traditional rendang soaked in coconut milk and 17 spices. Satay smothered in his secret peanut sauce. Sambal eggs that make homesick students smile.

Ayo Eat is the only Indonesian eatery in Victoria. "Ayo" means "come" in Indonesian. That's the whole pitch. Come eat.
