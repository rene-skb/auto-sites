# Wild Rose Tattoo Company — Panel Notes

## WHY critique
- **Steve Jobs — 7.4**
  - Honest and restrained, but the hero does not hit with enough conviction on first read.
  - The page feels considered, though it informs more than it compels.
- **Paul Graham — 7.8**
  - Good specificity, especially the named artists and private-studio framing.
  - Some copy still reads like positioning notes instead of the simplest possible argument.
- **David Ogilvy — 6.9**
  - The headline is elegant but not persuasive enough.
  - Trust signals exist, but they should land earlier and more explicitly.
- **Panel average:** 7.37
- **Next changes:**
  - Replace the hero headline with a more specific promise.
  - Tighten the hero copy into a clearer value prop.
  - Surface trust signals faster near the top of the page.

## WHAT critique
- **Don Norman — 7.5**
  - The page has trust and tone, but key facts are repeated instead of simplified.
  - The action path is still a little fragmented.
- **Steve Krug — 6.5**
  - Too many same-weight cards make the useful information harder to scan.
  - The hero promise is clear, but practical details should arrive faster.
- **Jakob Nielsen — 7.0**
  - Contact and visit details exist, though discoverability would improve with stronger chunking.
  - The utility layer should be more obvious than the decorative structure around it.
- **Panel average:** 7.00
- **Next changes:**
  - Create one tighter top summary block with address, hours, phone, artists, and booking method.
  - Reduce the hero headline scale slightly.
  - Use one dominant CTA and down-rank secondary actions.
  - Merge overlapping info panels into fewer clearer sections.

## HOW critique
- **Massimo Vignelli — 6.8**
  - The structure wants a stricter grid and fewer component variations.
  - Too many soft container treatments blur the hierarchy.
- **Erik Spiekermann — 7.4**
  - The serif-plus-sans pairing works, but body text in small modules needs more breathing room.
  - Contact and utility type should feel more assertive.
- **Dieter Rams — 6.5**
  - The page is honest, though not yet reduced enough.
  - Large radii, shadows, and repeated cards add noise.
- **Emil Kowalski — 8.0**
  - The overall mood is strong and motion-ready.
  - A smaller, more consistent motion system would lift the page without turning decorative.
- **Panel average:** 7.18
- **Next changes:**
  - Enforce a tighter grid rhythm and unify module spacing.
  - Reduce card styling, shadows, and radii.
  - Increase small-text readability.
  - Keep motion to a compact, repeatable reveal system.
