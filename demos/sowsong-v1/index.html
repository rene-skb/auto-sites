Meet the Maker

I design jewelry for people who value story over status.

Every piece starts with a conversation. What's the stone that speaks to you? What shape fits the life you're living? I work with salt-and-pepper diamonds, organic forms, and bezel settings that feel timeless without being traditional.

This work is dedicated to my grandfather. He taught me that the things worth keeping are the ones made with intention.

The Name

Sow Song: An Ode to Love

The name comes from two things: the act of sowing (planting, nurturing, watching something grow) and song (the thing that plays when words aren't enough). Together, they're about what love looks like when you're in it for the long haul.

I make heirloom pieces. Jewelry designed to outlast everything else.
