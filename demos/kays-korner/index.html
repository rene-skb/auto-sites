A Little Corner of Character

Kay's Korner is a vintage store in the heart of Cook Street Village. We carry a rotating collection of furniture, bric-a-brac, and collectibles — primarily vintage, always interesting.

From funky painted and distressed furniture to 60's retro pieces, mid-century finds, and all sorts of curious things in between. Our displays change regularly, each one thoughtfully themed to inspire your next treasure hunt.

Whether you're furnishing a home, hunting for a gift, or just love the thrill of the find — there's something here for you.
