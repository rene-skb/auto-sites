What We Make

Organic milk. Local fruit. Seasonal everything.

Ice cream, sherbet, frozen yogurt, vegan sorbet, ice pops — made in small batches from ingredients we know and trust.

Organic dairy. Fruit from local family farms. Figs and blackberries foraged from BC vines. The flavour ends when the season does. Follow our Instagram to know what's in the case right now.

The Origin

Made from the place it comes from.
