Our Story

Born From a Shared Dream

"We wanted to create a space where people could nurture their spirit and just connect — either with yourself or others."
— Wilson North, Co-owner

Wilson and Hanisa North always knew they wanted to work together. The dream had been there for years — a coffee shop where the neighbourhood could gather, connect, and just be. In 2025, they finally made it real.

Wilson, who grew up in New Zealand, brought with him the Māori concept of wairua — the idea that every person carries a spirit worth nurturing. It became the soul of the shop.

Since opening, the North Park community has embraced Wairua as their own. What started with zero expectations has become something genuinely special.

The Name

Wairua

/why · roo · a/

A Māori word meaning one's spirit — the essence that makes you, you. Wilson chose it because he wanted a place where people could nurture that part of themselves, whether through a quiet moment alone or a conversation with a stranger who becomes a friend.

"The community has entirely taken us in. It's a real privilege to bring some joy to the neighbourhood."
