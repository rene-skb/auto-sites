03 — The Truck

Meet Suzi

1991 Suzuki · 9 months · Built by Garrett & Bruce

She spent years in a storage locker in central Alberta. Garrett and his dad Bruce pulled her out and spent 9 months and multiple redesigns turning her into a mobile cafe. The essentials of your favourite third-wave cafe, built into a truck.
