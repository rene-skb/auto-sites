Our Story

Started at the farmers market, stayed for the neighborhood

Petal & Stem began in 2016 as a small stand at the Grand Army Plaza Greenmarket. Elena Marchetti — third-generation Italian, first-generation Brooklynite — started selling hand-tied bouquets from flowers she grew at a community garden in Red Hook.

Word spread. A few restaurant contracts turned into a storefront on 5th Avenue. Now, eight years in, we're a Park Slope fixture — the kind of shop where regulars walk in on Tuesdays just to see what's fresh.

We still source locally when the season allows. The rest comes from small farms we've worked with for years. Every stem gets chosen on purpose.
