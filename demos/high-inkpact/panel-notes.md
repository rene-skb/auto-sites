# Panel Notes — High INKpact Tattoo Studio

## WHY critique

### Steve Jobs — 7.8
- The dark, premium direction fits the category, but the first headline needed more conviction.
- The room has to feel calm and confident, not generic tattoo-shop edgy.
- Lead with the promise that the piece will still feel personal.

### Paul Graham — 7.4
- The page worked better once the copy dropped extra throat-clearing.
- The useful truth here is simple: clean studio, custom work, people feel comfortable.
- Keep the facts early and avoid writing around them.

### David Ogilvy — 7.6
- The strongest line is the one about bringing the idea and leaving with the piece.
- Review framing should stay short so the real quotes do the persuasion.
- Utility bar needs harder-working labels.

**Panel average:** 7.6

**Next changes for Lucy**
1. Make the hero claim sharper and more specific.
2. Trim the review intro copy.
3. Tighten the quick facts labels so they scan faster.

## WHAT critique

### Don Norman — 7.8
- The structure is clear once the utility rail lands right after the hero.
- The useful actions are obvious: call, open maps, read proof.
- One or two repeated phrases could still be trimmed.

### Steve Krug — 7.6
- I know what this is fast, which is the bar.
- CTA labels should say exactly what happens next.
- The page scans well because the major sections each answer one question.

### Jakob Nielsen — 7.5
- The review metadata is helpful and specific.
- The contact block is appropriately practical.
- Avoid making users parse too many similar comfort/custom claims in a row.

**Panel average:** 7.63

**Next changes for Lucy**
1. Simplify repeated phrases in the hero and story sections.
2. Keep CTA labels literal.
3. Preserve the strong scan path: hero, quick facts, proof, visit.

## HOW critique

### Massimo Vignelli — 7.8
- The page has a coherent visual system and enough restraint.
- The serif headline does the heavy lifting well.
- Contrast and spacing should stay disciplined, not theatrical.

### Erik Spiekermann — 8.0
- The typography pairing feels right for premium, editorial tattoo work.
- The utility labels and review metadata give the sans a real job.
- Keep body copy short so the type can breathe.

### Dieter Rams — 7.7
- Good reduction. Nothing major feels ornamental.
- The page improves when each sentence earns its place.
- Resist adding more decoration just because the category invites it.

### Emil Kowalski — 8.1
- Motion should stay invisible: reveal on scroll, gentle transitions, responsive button press.
- No hover gimmicks on images.
- The dark palette benefits from subtle pacing, not spectacle.

**Panel average:** 7.9

**Next changes for Lucy**
1. Tighten contrast in the hero and proof sections.
2. Keep motion to reveal + button feedback only.
3. Cut any sentence that feels like it is repeating the same idea.
4. Preserve the premium dark rhythm without adding visual noise.
