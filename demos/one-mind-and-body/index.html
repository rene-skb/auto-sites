About Linda

West coast roots. Whole-person approach.

One Mind and Body was founded in 2010 in Toronto by Linda Watson, a west coast woman with a deep passion for healthy living. Now back on Vancouver Island, she brings over 15 years of experience in personal training, kickboxing, and holistic wellness to Greater Victoria.

Linda's approach goes beyond sets and reps. She believes in the mind-body connection, combining physical training with mindful movement, nutrition guidance, and genuine personal empowerment. She's known for pushing her clients hard while keeping things real, supportive, and fun.

Whether she's leading a Buddha Camp class at sunrise or coaching someone through their first kickboxing session, Linda meets everyone exactly where they are.
