# Panel Notes — Barbershop Patisserie

## Creative direction (pre-build)

### Steve Jobs — WHY / conviction — 8.0
- The page should make you hungry in 5 seconds.
- Lead with the food people cross town for, not the founder biography.
- Keep the page feeling expensive but not precious.

### Paul Graham — WHY / clarity — 7.5
- Say exactly what the place sells.
- Don't hide behind pastry-language. Use the actual item names.
- Keep the sections short. Appetite beats explanation.

### David Ogilvy — WHY / copy — 8.0
- "Puff pastry, sausage rolls, and cakes worth crossing town for" is specific enough to work.
- Let the review names carry the proof.
- One strong owner quote is better than a whole brand manifesto.

**WHY average:** 7.83
**Build change for v1:** keep giant-display-type hero, strengthen item specificity, keep the opening paragraph practical.

---

## WHAT panel (v1 review)

### Don Norman — 7.5
- The page answers the basic questions quickly.
- The quick facts row helps the walk-in use case.
- Good restraint on menu detail since pricing is unverified.

### Steve Krug — 8.0
- I know what it is, where it is, and what to order without thinking.
- The sections scan fast.
- The nav labels are plain, which is correct.

### Jakob Nielsen — 7.5
- Contrast is solid.
- Buttons say what happens next.
- Review attribution is compliant and useful.

**WHAT average:** 7.67
**Build change for v2:** tighten the visit copy, keep the CTA labels literal, avoid adding any decorative section.

---

## HOW panel (v2 review)

### Massimo Vignelli — 7.5
- The typography has enough authority for a pastry shop with this name.
- The section rhythm is clean.
- The giant hero works because the copy is short.

### Erik Spiekermann — 7.0
- Body copy is readable and not over-styled.
- Good decision to keep only two font families.
- The gallery captions should stay small and plain, not clever.

### Dieter Rams — 8.0
- Nothing obviously redundant.
- The review section earns its space because the quotes name specific items.
- No fake map embed. Good.

**HOW average:** 7.50
**Build change for v3:** keep the page lean, reduce any wording that sounds too polished, and leave the images to do the work.

---

## Final self-review — 8.1
- Strong appetite from the first screen.
- Good use of real photo assets.
- Facts are visible early.
- Proof section is specific instead of generic.
- The page feels like a bakery with swagger, not a generic patisserie template.
