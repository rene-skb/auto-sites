The Story

"Don't be afraid to ask. Worst case, you're told no and you find another way."

Marisa first learned to sew from her grandma as a kid. She took sewing lessons through school, tried different careers, and eventually found upholstery — and loved it. She apprenticed for four years in Duncan before opening MINT on Pembroke Street in 2021.

Her studio sits on Pembroke Street in what she calls Victoria's "upholstery district" — near Gala Fabrics and McGeachie's Foam, surrounded by the resources and knowledge that make the work better. If a chair can be saved, it should be. Marisa and Joe will walk you through the options and deliver something that looks better than the day you bought it.

The Name

Mint Condition Isn't a Starting Point. It's the Destination.

Every piece that leaves the studio goes out in mint condition. That's the promise. Not close enough, not good enough — mint. It's why Marisa won't send something home unless it's right.

What We Do

Give It Another Life

01

Full Reupholstery

Strip it down, rebuild the padding, recover in your choice of fabric. Dining chairs, armchairs, sofas, benches — anything with a seat and a story.

02

Slip Covers & Repairs

Custom slip covers that fit so perfectly you'd never know they weren't original. We also patch, mend, and restore covers that have seen better days.

03

Cushion & Foam Work

New high-density foam cuts, cushion refills, and reshaping. Bring back the comfort without replacing the whole piece.

04

Fabric Selection

Not sure what material? Marisa walks you through the options — durability, texture, colour — without pressure. Get it right the first time.
