About Moov

Born with one mission:
bring real street dance
to Ottawa.

Founded in 2018 by Alea "Didi" de Castro and Arnaldo "Effect" Betancourt Silva. Two competitive performers who decided Ottawa needed a home for hip hop and street dance taught with cultural depth, not just choreography.

Today Moov runs weekly classes, workshops, school programs, and live performances across two Ottawa locations. Bilingual. All levels. Spring 2026 registration is open now.
