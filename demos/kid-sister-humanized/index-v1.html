About

The flavors change when the fruit does.

Christen makes ice cream from whatever's growing nearby. Organic dairy from island farms. Blackberries she picked herself. Figs from somebody's backyard. If the strawberries are done for the season, the roasted strawberry is done too.

The menu lives on Instagram because it changes that often. What's in the case today depends on what came in this week.

Women-owned. LGBTQ+ friendly. On Esquimalt Road.

The Name

The one who shows up with something sweet and makes everything a little better.

What We Make

Everything from scratch, nothing from a mix.

Ice Cream Organic dairy, local cream, flavors that rotate with the seasons. Scooped into waffle cones or packed into pints.
Popsicles Fruit cream pops and sorbets. Some have edible flowers pressed into them. They look almost too good to eat.
Vegan Sorbet Dairy-free, real fruit, same local ingredients. Just without the cream.
Pints Take it home. Also available at stockists around Victoria and the island.
