About VilleVelo

"City bike."
That's the name.

VilleVelo is French for "city bike" — a name that fits perfectly in Portland, where cycling and neighborhood life are the same thing. The bake shop sits on NE Prescott, opens at seven, and closes when everything sells out.

The gallery wall behind the counter tells its own story — layers of photos, art, and frames that make the space feel like someone's home. That's the point.
