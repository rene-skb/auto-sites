# Famiglia Tattoo Victoria — Panel Notes

## WHY critique

### Steve Jobs — 7.5
- Strong taste and visual restraint. Premium, calm, intentional.
- The page explains itself a little too much. Conviction drops when the copy over-justifies.
- The core promise is right, but the first screen needs one sharper idea.

### Paul Graham — 7.0
- Solves a real user problem: artist fit, room details, and booking clarity before commitment.
- Slightly verbose in places. The page sometimes explains the same point twice.
- The real spine is reducing uncertainty before booking. Everything should point there.

### David Ogilvy — 8.0
- Tone and typography feel branded without sounding generic.
- Some lines are elegant but a little abstract for a local-service landing page.
- Specifics build trust here. Lead with them harder.

**Panel average:** 7.5

**Next changes**
1. Sharpen the hero into a more direct promise.
2. Remove conceptual repetition around clarity and trust.
3. Make CTAs more outcome-driven.
4. Add a tighter quick-proof row near the top.

## WHAT critique

### Don Norman — 7.5
- The booking path is there, but the page benefits when the action sequence is even more explicit.
- Access and rights content are useful, just slightly dense in places.
- The structure is solid and feels trustworthy.

### Steve Krug — 8.0
- Easy to scan overall, especially the artist cards.
- The page gets better when headings say exactly what a section is for.
- Repeating the same CTA language helps people move faster.

### Jakob Nielsen — 7.3
- Good readability and clear sections.
- Some headings were a little poetic relative to the practical content below them.
- The visit and booking basics need the most literal language.

**Panel average:** 7.6

**Next changes**
1. Make the booking flow read as a simple sequence.
2. Use plainer section headings where utility matters most.
3. Keep CTA labels consistent everywhere.
4. Tighten any remaining explanatory copy in the utility sections.

## HOW critique

### Massimo Vignelli — 8.2
- The grid is disciplined and the pacing is strong.
- The strongest opportunity is removing small pieces that do not add much to structure.

### Erik Spiekermann — 7.8
- Typography feels coherent and branded.
- Body contrast and text size can come up a little more on dark panels.

### Dieter Rams — 8.0
- The page is restrained and mostly clear.
- One less utility card would make the practical section cleaner.

### Emil Kowalski — 8.1
- Motion should stay quiet, with reveals that support the hierarchy.
- The hero image could use a clearer tie-in to the booking story.

**Panel average:** 8.03

**Next changes**
1. Raise contrast and body size slightly on dark surfaces.
2. Add a clearer caption or note on the hero image.
3. Trim one non-essential utility element.
4. Keep motion to subtle reveal and button feedback only.
