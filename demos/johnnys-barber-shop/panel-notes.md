# Panel Notes — Johnny's Barber + Shop

## WHY critique
- **Steve Jobs — 7.5**
  - Premium feel and strong taste, the page already feels considered.
  - Hero idea needs more inevitability and less phrasing.
- **Paul Graham — 7.0**
  - Real substance is there: locations, pricing, bios, photos.
  - Core pitch still needs to answer why this shop and why now more directly.
- **David Ogilvy — 6.5**
  - Headline is too literary for a landing page.
  - Strong selling proof exists, but too much of it arrives after the fold.
- **Panel average:** 7.0
- **Next changes:**
  1. Rewrite the hero headline to be more direct and benefit-led.
  2. Tighten the hero subhead around the real differentiators.
  3. Move stronger proof into the above-the-fold area.

## WHAT critique
- **Don Norman — 7.8**
  - Strong basic mental model: hero, locations, services, proof, shop, CTA.
  - Too many similar cards make decisions slower than they should be.
- **Steve Krug — 7.2**
  - Most question marks disappear fast.
  - Hero and utility content still need tighter chunking and stronger next-step emphasis.
- **Jakob Nielsen — 7.5**
  - Real photos, concrete prices, and named barbers build trust well.
  - Interactive emphasis is uneven, and repeated CTAs need a clearer primary path.
- **Panel average:** 7.5
- **Next changes:**
  1. Make booking the obvious primary action.
  2. Shorten and chunk the hero and utility copy for faster scanning.
  3. Differentiate the 2 location cards with clearer comparison points.

## HOW critique
- **Massimo Vignelli — 8.4**
  - Strong editorial restraint and clear sectioning.
  - System still has slightly too many radius and module moods.
- **Erik Spiekermann — 8.1**
  - Macro typography works well.
  - Smaller supporting text and CTA emphasis need sharpening.
- **Dieter Rams — 8.6**
  - Useful, calm, and honest overall.
  - Decorative framing can still be simplified.
- **Emil Kowalski — 7.9**
  - Warm palette and restrained polish feel strong.
  - Motion language should be made more explicit and consistent.
- **Panel average:** 8.25
- **Next changes:**
  1. Tighten the page to 2 main module styles.
  2. Increase readability in dense text areas.
  3. Align sections more consistently to the grid.
  4. Make the motion system clearer and more uniform.
