The Name

"Sow Song"

My Grandpa Soby was a banjo player. Sow Song was the tune he'd sing to us grandkids. Playful. Full of love.

When I make jewelry, I think about connection. Each piece holds meaning throughout a lifetime. A token of love, the way his song is for me.
