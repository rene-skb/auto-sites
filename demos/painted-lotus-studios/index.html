Painted Lotus has been part of downtown Victoria since 2009. Not a chain, not a flash shop. A collective of seven working artists — each with their own style, voice, and portfolio.

You're not getting whoever's available. You're choosing your artist. Browse their work, reach out directly, and build your tattoo with someone whose style genuinely speaks to you.
