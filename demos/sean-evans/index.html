About

Sean Evans is a sound design artist based in Vancouver, BC. His work lives between ambient, drone, and minimalism, built slowly at Kiklo Studios over months of patient recording.

Movements took thirteen months. Recorded at Kiklo Studios from January 2019 to February 2020, mixed by Jordan Esau, mastered by Joshua Stevenson at Otic Mastering. Saxophone by Paolo Carcamo. Pressed on 180-gram vinyl by Poly Custom Records in North Vancouver.
