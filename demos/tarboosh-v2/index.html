My Grandfather's Recipes

He wore a crimson tarboosh and cooked from memory. Now his family recipes travel from Cairo's streets to a food truck in Esquimalt.

Call to Order — 236-464-9111
The Truck

Nothing shortcuts

The pita is made by hand, every single day. The spice blends are mixed from memory, not a supplier's catalogue. The falafel are fried to order because that's the only way they stay moist.

This is Egyptian street food the way it's supposed to taste. Bold, honest, and worth the drive to Esquimalt.
