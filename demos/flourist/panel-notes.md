# Flourist Panel Notes

## WHY critique
### Steve Jobs — 8.2
- The page lands once it stops circling the idea and just says it: fresh flour should taste alive.
- The loaf photo and the in-house milling claim feel inevitable together. Keep that pairing in front.

### Paul Graham — 7.8
- The real differentiator is not “craft.” It is freshness, oils, grain integrity, and the fact that Flourist tells people to refrigerate the flour.
- Cut any sentence that explains the same thesis twice.

### David Ogilvy — 8.4
- Strongest selling points are concrete: Commercial Street, in-house milling, pizza at 5PM, sourdough class, and named customer proof.
- Lead with the product payoff, then stack specifics.

**WHY panel average:** 8.13

### Next changes
1. Tighten the hero support copy and get to the bakery offer faster.
2. Reduce abstract explanation above the fold.
3. Keep the loaf image and the in-house milling claim as the central pairing.

## WHAT critique
### Don Norman — 8.0
- The scan path is much clearer once the page answers “what is this?” before “why does it matter?”
- Visit details and class details now feel easier to parse.

### Steve Krug — 7.7
- The visitor can understand the page quickly, but repeated “why it matters” lines still show up mid-scroll.
- Keep every section to one job.

### Jakob Nielsen — 8.1
- Labels and card groupings are stronger now.
- Lower sections still benefit from simpler intros and less repetition.

**WHAT panel average:** 7.93

### Next changes
1. Sharpen section intros so each one says something new.
2. Reduce repeated freshness language in the middle of the page.
3. Keep classes, proof, and visit cards highly scannable.

## HOW critique
### Massimo Vignelli — 8.1
- The Newsreader and Instrument Sans pairing is doing real work now.
- Warm paper backgrounds and the dark utility block create distinct chapters without breaking the system.

### Erik Spiekermann — 8.0
- Better rhythm and hierarchy, but body copy still needs one last trim pass.
- The serif voice should carry the emotional weight. Let the sans stay quiet and functional.

### Dieter Rams — 7.8
- Honest and useful. Better once excess explanation is removed.
- Remove anything that repeats a proof point already established elsewhere.

### Emil Kowalski — 8.4
- Motion should stay restrained: section reveal, staggered cards, button press state.
- No decorative image motion. The photography and pacing are already enough.

**HOW panel average:** 8.08

### Next changes
1. Trim one more layer of explanation in the mid-page sections.
2. Keep motion to reveal and feedback only.
3. Let the review feature feel like proof, not decoration.
