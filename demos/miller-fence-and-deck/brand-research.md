# Brand research — Miller Fence & Deck

## Business
- Miller Fence & Deck
- Fence, gate, deck, railing, and outdoor-structure contractor
- Sidney / Greater Victoria / Saanich / Langford / Westshore / Vancouver Island, BC
- Website: https://www.millerfence.ca/
- Phone: (250) 656-7529
- Email: info@millerfence.ca

## Source-backed signals
- Around/over 35 years in business appears on homepage/contact/footer language.
- Family owned and operated appears in site header/footer.
- Core services: cedar, pressure treated, bamboo, chain link, steel, aluminum, pre-built cedar fence panels, decks, waterproofing, railings, glass/aluminum/picket railing, custom gates, SLEEKFENCE modern horizontal fencing.
- Site speaks directly to deer, pets, security, privacy, unreliable contractors, warranty, and free quote.
- Contact form asks for approximate budget buckets: 0-5k, 5-10k, 10-20k, 20k+.
- Official testimonials mention reliability, budget/time, aluminum and ornamental fencing, professionalism, clean work areas, railings, and a solid deck.

## Creative angle
Miller is a practical Greater Victoria outdoor-boundary company. The customer is not buying “a fence” in the abstract. They are trying to solve a boundary problem: deer in the garden, pets escaping, privacy, security, old fence failure, a deck that needs to be safe, or railings that finish the space.

## Visual direction
- Use real official project photos. The black horizontal fence and elevated/composite deck assets are the strongest.
- Palette: charred cedar, weathered cream, steel blue/green, muted sage, warm sawdust.
- Typography: Fraunces for confident editorial display, Inter for utility and quote/CTA clarity.
- Hero pattern: giant-display-type with bottom project-photo band. This avoids repeating the last three contractor hero systems and lets the fence line become the visual rhythm.

## Risk notes
- No prices beyond the contact form budget buckets.
- Do not overclaim “best warranty” except where source says it; better phrase as source-backed warranty positioning.
- Do not invent address.
- Use exact Google listing URL only if needed; avoid generic address links.
- Blog/news remnants are placeholder content and should not appear.
