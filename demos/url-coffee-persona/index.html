The Place

Skateboard
stools.
Tech pun.
Good coffee.

Ethan Choi and Zoey Jung opened URL Coffee on Broadway in March 2021. The stools are made from skateboard decks. The name is a quiet tech pun. The light is always good.

Eater Seattle called it "mid-century modern with a note of punkish whimsy."
