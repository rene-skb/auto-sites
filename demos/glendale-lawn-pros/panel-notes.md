# Panel notes - Glendale Lawn Pros

## Creative direction
- Build around the clearest real benefit: Glendale homeowners get their Saturday back.
- Keep the site phone-first. The current official site and search snippets both point to calling 818-423-4133 for a quote.
- Use official lawn/yard images only. No fake storefront, no invented crew photos.

## WHY critique

### Steve Jobs - 8.0
- The “Take back Saturday” direction is the right emotional button for a recurring lawn service.
- The first hero image felt too park-like. Put the work in the hero so the promise feels earned.
- Phone CTA should feel like the product button.

### Paul Graham - 7.8
- Clearer than the original site, but remove all internal “current site” framing.
- Keep the copy grounded in chores, schedules, and time. Don’t make the lawn mystical.
- The proof boxes need real meaning or they should go.

### David Ogilvy - 8.1
- “Saturday belongs to you” is a stronger ad line than a service list.
- The service names are plain enough to sell.
- Use the phone number in the CTA itself.

**WHY panel average:** 7.97

**Next changes**
1. Swap hero to mower-action photo.
2. Make the phone CTA dominant and number-specific.
3. Remove demo-facing copy.
4. Replace vague trust box language with concrete quote / schedule / family-owned facts.

## WHAT critique

### Don Norman - 8.1
- The structure is understandable: hero, services, process, reviews, contact.
- Contact gives enough information without forcing a form.
- Make the “How it works” section read as steps, not as decorative cards.

### Steve Krug - 8.0
- A visitor knows what to do: call.
- Service cards scan cleanly.
- Keep secondary actions quiet. Phone wins.

### Jakob Nielsen - 7.8
- The 4-column service and step grids need tablet handling before mobile.
- Same-treatment reviews follow the rule and reduce visual noise.
- Avoid using tiny explanatory text as the only source of trust.

**WHAT panel average:** 7.97

**Next changes**
1. Add tablet 2-column grid behavior.
2. Keep service descriptions short.
3. Make contact and hours easy to scan.

## HOW critique

### Massimo Vignelli - 7.9
- The grid is simple and the dark/light chaptering gives structure.
- Condensed typography is strong but close to overused. Keep headings short.
- The rounded cards are acceptable because the palette stays restrained.

### Erik Spiekermann - 8.1
- Barlow Condensed fits contractor signage better than another editorial serif.
- Body type is readable and not too small.
- Watch all-caps density; short phrases only.

### Dieter Rams - 7.8
- Remove anything that reads like a stats bar or decorative trust strip.
- The fake form should be an “ask about” panel, not pretend inputs.
- The page can stay simple because the service is simple.

### Emil Kowalski - 8.2
- Motion should be scroll reveal only, with button feedback.
- No image hover effects.
- Use reduced-motion fallback and exact transform/opacity transitions.

**HOW panel average:** 8.00

**Next changes**
1. Keep motion restrained: reveal and button press only.
2. Ensure no image hover zoom appears.
3. Final copy audit for banned patterns and unsourced claims.
