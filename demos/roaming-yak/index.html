The Story

"Roaming Yak is a fantastic spot for authentic Tibetan cuisine and a truly memorable food experience." Nayan S. · Dec 2024 · Via TripAdvisor

They Grew Up Making This Food. Now They Make It for Calgary.

Roaming Yak opened in late November 2025. The family behind it didn't learn Tibetan cooking in a restaurant. They learned it at home — momos, shabhaley, thukpa. The food of the Himalayas, made the way it's always been made.

Every meal starts with complimentary butter tea. Salted, rich, nothing like what you expect. That's the point. Most of what's on this menu will be a first for you.
