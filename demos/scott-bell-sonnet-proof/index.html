Work at scale.

Systems work, under real constraints, with products that had to keep moving.

Strike Senior Product Designer 2021–2023

A complete redesign for 1.5 million users in 65 countries.

Strike is a Bitcoin payments app that handles real money for over 1.5 million users. I redesigned the entire product, 450+ screens, with five months to do it.

The hard part wasn’t the scale. It was making every surface work across currencies, regulatory contexts, and user expectations that changed by market, without creating a product that felt fragmented.

AIOZ Network Head of Design 2023–2024

Eight products. Market cap from $13M to $1.38B.

AIOZ is a decentralized AI and media infrastructure network. As Head of Design I led across eight simultaneous products, from staking platforms to AI marketplaces to streaming infrastructure, while the company scaled roughly 100× in market cap.

The challenge was not just speed. It was holding the design quality together while each product was changing at a different rate for a different audience.

Fountain Lead Product Designer 2022–2023

Full rebuild in under five months. Apple featured it twice.

Fountain is a Bitcoin podcasting app where listeners earn while they listen. The whole product needed rebuilding: new architecture, new design system, new everything. We shipped in five months.

Apple featured the result twice. The semantic design system I built gave the team a shared language, which meant new features could ship without re-litigating the basics every sprint.

KOHO Product Designer, Employee #1 2015–2018

Employee #1. Zero to 100,000 users and an $800M valuation.

KOHO is a Canadian neobank. I was the first employee and the first designer. No design team, no existing practice, no product language to inherit. I built the design function from scratch and helped take the product from concept to 100,000+ users.

The company later reached an $800M valuation. A lot of that later scale rested on decisions made in those first two years, when the system was still being invented.

Scientist brain.
Design hands.

I design from first principles. Start with what’s actually happening for the user, find the smallest intervention that changes it, then build the system that makes it repeatable.

I studied microbiology before design school. It turns out they train the same instinct: look closely, find the mechanism, change the right variable.

Based in Victoria, BC. Right now I’m focused on AI-native product design, especially interfaces where the AI is doing real work and the human still needs to stay oriented.
