## Notes and risk handling

- The official site says “more than 40 years” in one place and “over 26 years” in the FAQ. The final visible page avoids numeric years to prevent a conflict.
- The final page does not invent prices, license numbers, insurance claims, WCB/WorkSafe claims, awards, address, or a Google Maps link.
- Hazardous-material copy is deliberately worded around identifying/testing/qualified handling where required. It does not claim Pross personally performs licensed abatement beyond what the source supports.
- Images are official site assets, but the demo does not caption them as completed Pross projects unless the source explicitly supports that.
