Our Process

From scratch means from scratch

Most shops in Canada buy a pre-made base and add flavour. We cook and age our own from organic dairy, then churn it with fruit we picked that week.

We forage elderflower, pick berries from local farms, and take plums from backyard trees. What's ripe is what's on the menu. The whole thing happens in our kitchen on Esquimalt Road.

About Kid Sister

Started with a freezer and a kei truck

Now we have a shop on Esquimalt Road, a growing list of stockists, and a menu that changes every week based on what's ripe.

The fruit in your scoop comes from farms we visit. The dairy comes from cows we know. We work with local growers because that's how you get strawberries that taste like strawberries.
