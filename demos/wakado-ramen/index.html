The Menu

Pick your bowl.

No full menu here. Just the bowls people keep coming back for.

Most popular
Chili Goma
Pork bone broth, sesame paste, Szechuan chili oil. Rich, thick, properly spicy. The one everyone orders.
Mala Tonkotsu
Szechuan peppercorn broth. The numbing builds slowly and stays. Medium heat, numbing-forward. One of the most distinctive bowls in the city.
Tonkotsu Black
Deep pork bone broth, black garlic oil, chashu pork, bamboo shoots, nori. The classic — done right. Order the extra egg.
Spicy Miso
Fermented miso tare with house chili blend and pork broth. Real heat that builds. People call this the best in Calgary. Strong words for a miso.
Wakado Shoyu
Soy tare, light pork broth, truffle, chashu, and a perfectly marinated egg. The subtlest bowl here — clean, umami-forward, no heat.
Goma Mazemen
No broth. Thick sesame sauce, ground pork, Szechuan pepper, house noodles. Dense, savoury, chewy. Different from everything else on the menu.
