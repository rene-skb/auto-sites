The Source

The fruit comes first. Everything else follows.

Blackberries from island hedgerows. Figs picked by hand. Organic dairy from farms you could drive to in twenty minutes. Every flavour starts with what's ripe right now.

Women-owned. LGBTQ+ friendly. Every batch made by hand in a tiny Esquimalt shop where the menu lives on Instagram because it changes faster than any website could keep up.

What We Make

Scoops, pops, and things you won't find anywhere else.

Ice Cream

Roasted Strawberry. Blackberry Fig. Sea Salt Caramel. Hand-churned with organic dairy and whatever the island is growing this week. Scoops and pints.

Popsicles

Fruit pops and cream pops pressed with real edible flowers. The sunflower pop is the one people photograph before they eat.

Seasonal Specials

Dorayaki ice cream sandwiches. Kombucha floats with local kiwi colada. Matcha and black sesame scoops. The menu follows the seasons, and sometimes Japan.

Vegan Sorbet

Whole fruit, nothing else. The Matcha Lemon has a following of its own.

Right Now

The case changes. The Instagram tells you what's in it.

Cherry blossom season brought dorayaki sandwiches and black sesame sundaes. Spring means strawberry everything. Next month, who knows.
