A Welding
Torch.

Matt Phillips welded his own fermenters from stainless steel in a non-descript industrial unit in Esquimalt.

He did everything himself. Brewing. Packaging. Delivering. He slept at the brewery.

Twenty-plus years later, Phillips spans almost an entire city block in Victoria's Old Town.

The Phillips Way It's amazing what some beer nerds can accomplish with a little creativity and a welding torch. We've weathered catastrophic beer floods, imploding tanks, and a few address changes. We've always kept it unconventional.

Grain to Glass

One of Canada's Only
Craft Malt Houses

In 2015, Phillips launched their own malting plant, one of the only craft brewers in Canada to malt their own barley. This lets them work directly with local Vancouver Island farmers, reduce environmental emissions, and push creative boundaries with every recipe.

It's not just about better beer. Phillips has monitored their CO2 lifecycle analysis for over a decade, installed a mash press to cut barley consumption, and built a CO2 reclaim plant. Green beer isn't just for St. Paddy's Day.
