- CTA: get a free bid / project estimate

## Brand read
- Vibe: practical, soil-first, direct, experienced. The strongest differentiator is process honesty: pick-axe 6 to 8 inches, rototill, spread 3 inches of organic compost, rototill again.
- Colors: lawn green from logo and sod photos, dark soil brown, warm off-white.
- Typography direction: sturdy condensed sans for display, readable humanist sans for body. Avoid polished spa landscaping energy. This should feel like a contractor who can explain soil prep without flinching.
- Hero idea: “Start with the soil.” That is specific to RototillerGuy and sharper than another generic lawn hero.

## Creative direction panel
### Steve Jobs
- Lead with the process, not the menu of services. “Start with the soil” is the conviction.
- Show the before/after as a proof moment, not decoration.
- Score direction: 8.1

### Paul Graham
- Cut the SEO sludge. Say what Mike actually does: digs deeper, adds compost, helps people avoid bad installs.
- Keep paragraphs short and let reviews do the human work.
- Score direction: 8.0

### David Ogilvy
- The strongest sales fact is the 6 to 8 inch prep plus 3 inch compost layer. That sounds believable because it is specific.
- CTA should ask for a free bid, not “learn more.”
- Score direction: 8.2

## Rotation choices
- Hero pattern: asymmetric-collage, different from recent dark-immersive, split-image-right, editorial-spread.
- Typography: Barlow Condensed + Source Sans 3, different from recent pairings.
- Review layout: three same-treatment “field note” cards in a horizontal/stacked soil-paper block, not a featured-card checkerboard.
- Visit/contact layout: service-area command panel with phone cards and one verified Maps business listing link.
- Stats: no-stats. Numbers are woven into copy.
