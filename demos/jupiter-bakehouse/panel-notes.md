# Panel Notes — Jupiter Bakehouse

## WHY critique

**Steve Jobs — 7.5**
- Lead with the split that makes the place memorable: bread and pastry in the same breath.
- The page feels grounded once the food lands first.
- Keep the tone restrained. The bakery already has taste.

**Paul Graham — 7.0**
- The useful thing is simple: what it is, where it is, when to go.
- Press facts are stronger than warmed-over brand language.
- Cut any sentence that sounds like a brochure.

**David Ogilvy — 7.5**
- “Long-fermented bread. French pastry.” is the right kind of headline, short and specific.
- The supporting copy should stay concrete, names of products beat adjectives.
- Keep the lead on product, not biography.

**WHY panel average:** 7.33

**Next changes**
- Tighten the lead so the value proposition lands inside one breath.
- Keep bread + pastry as the central idea through the first two sections.
- Remove anything that sounds polite instead of specific.

## WHAT critique

**Don Norman — 7.5**
- The page now answers the first-time visitor's main questions fast.
- Practical details are visible early enough to reduce friction.
- Product guidance is clearer once it becomes a short list.

**Steve Krug — 8.0**
- The scan path is obvious.
- Quick facts under the hero do real work.
- “Three easy calls” makes the menu section effortless.

**Jakob Nielsen — 7.0**
- Contrast is solid enough, but practical information must stay above the fold on mobile too.
- Keep body copy short and avoid extra caveats in visible text.
- One primary action per section is cleaner than button clutter.

**WHAT panel average:** 7.5

**Next changes**
- Keep the quick-facts strip tight and readable on mobile.
- Preserve short paragraphs and single-purpose actions.
- Trim any explanatory copy that can become a list item.

## HOW critique

**Massimo Vignelli — 7.0**
- The structure is coherent once the cards use the same quiet language.
- The page works better with fewer decorative moves.
- Keep the image moments limited.

**Erik Spiekermann — 7.5**
- Newsreader carries the bakery tone without becoming precious.
- Work Sans keeps the information readable.
- The hierarchy is clearer when the section intros stay disciplined.

**Dieter Rams — 7.0**
- Nothing extra should be added from here.
- The simple card rhythm is enough.
- If a sentence doesn't help someone visit or order, cut it.

**Emil Kowalski — 7.5**
- Motion should stay restrained: hero line stagger, section reveal, button press.
- The page doesn't need anything more theatrical.
- Make sure everything is visible without JavaScript.

**HOW panel average:** 7.25

**Next changes**
- Keep motion limited to reveal + active states.
- Preserve one accent color and avoid extra texture.
- Final pass should focus on copy tightening and deployment hygiene.
