The Edit

Not thrift.
A point of view.

Botched is a curated vintage shop on Victoria's Fort Street, where every piece is hand-selected for quality, era, and attitude. Men's and women's, from 90s designer to premium denim, leather, and the kind of pieces you'd find in a stylist's personal archive.

Located inside Botched Shop at 1044A Fort Street, in the heart of Victoria's vintage corridor. New drops land on Instagram first. Worldwide shipping available.
