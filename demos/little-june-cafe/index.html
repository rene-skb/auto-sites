The Story

Fernwood's living room since day one

Little June is the kind of place where regulars have a usual and newcomers feel like regulars. Tucked into Fernwood Square, it's small on purpose — every seat earned, every coffee made with beans roasted in house.

The menu is tight: baked eggs, breakfast sandwiches, pastries that don't last till noon. Specialty drinks like the maple Shay Shay have a quiet cult following. Come early or make peace with the alley patio.
