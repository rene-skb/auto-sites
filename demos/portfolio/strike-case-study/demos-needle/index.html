The Craft

One woman.
One shop. Extraordinary detail.

For over two decades, Lilia has built a reputation as Victoria's go-to seamstress. European-trained precision applied to every garment that comes through the door — from wedding dresses to worn-out zippers.

It's a one-person operation by design. No hand-offs, no assembly line. When you bring your garment to Lilia, she's the one who measures, cuts, and stitches it. That's the promise.

Customers don't just come back — they send their friends, their families, their wedding parties. Trust earned stitch by stitch.

"It's a beautiful jacket. Very well-made, very expensive," said Lilia Chtcherbakova, at her tiny tailoring shop on Hillside Avenue.

What We Do

European Precision,
Every Time

01

Alterations

Hems, waist adjustments, sleeve shortening, tapering — clothing altered to fit your body perfectly. All fabrics and garment types.

02

Suits & Formalwear

Expert tailoring for suits, blazers, dress pants, and formalwear. European training means understanding structure and drape.

03

Bridal & Special Occasion

Wedding dresses, bridesmaid gowns, and formal alterations handled with the care and precision these garments demand.

04

Repairs & Restoration

Zippers replaced, seams repaired, buttons, patches, and fabric restoration. No need to bring your own zipper — we carry all types.

05

Home Textiles

Curtains hemmed, cushion covers made or repaired, custom household textiles. The same precision applied beyond the wardrobe.

06

Custom Work

Custom fittings and made-to-measure pieces. Discuss your vision with Lilia and she'll bring European dressmaking tradition to your project.

The Name

Finding the needle in the haystack

In a city full of options, finding the right seamstress can feel impossible — like searching for a needle in a haystack. Lilia named her shop as a promise: you've found her. The search is over.

A small shop on Hillside. A master seamstress from Eastern Europe. Twenty-five years of quiet, exceptional work. Sometimes the best things are the hardest to find.
