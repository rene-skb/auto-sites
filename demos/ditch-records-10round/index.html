The Story

Born from obsession.

If it wasn't for the Beatles basically dictating the life of one 15-year-old music nerd, Ditch Records would just be a phantom of Victoria's imagination.

That obsession became Fort Street's most packed, most loved, most chaotic record store. The aisles are tight. The bins are deep. The staff will go to absurd lengths to find what you're looking for. If they can't, they'll order it.

We're not curated. We're comprehensive. Punk, jazz, hip-hop, electronic, metal, folk, classical, whatever you're into. 10,000+ records and counting. Come find what you didn't know you needed.
