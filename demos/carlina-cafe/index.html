The Story

He's Been Getting Ready for This His Whole Life

Charly Cardilicchia grew up watching his grandparents cook. Their recipes — the sugo, the meatballs, the ricotta — stayed with him. When he opened Carlina Cafe, he didn't reinvent them. He just found a kitchen of his own.

His father John, who watched Charly work himself into the ground getting the cafe ready, put it simply: "He's just taken on the tradition from my mom and my dad and added his own stuff to it."

That tradition is on every plate.

The Name

Carlo. Lina.
Carlina.

Charly's grandfather was Carlo. His grandmother was Lina. The cafe's name is both of them — and every recipe on the menu carries that same inheritance. When you sit down, you're eating what they made.
