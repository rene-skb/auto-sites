The name

A fez, a family, and a food truck.

Halah Abouhassan's grandfather wore a tarboosh — a bright red fez — as part of his Egyptian military uniform. That hat is now painted on the side of her truck. It's not just branding. It's her family.

Halah cooks everything herself. Her son Basil is her partner in the business. "Honestly, I'm just amazed with how much she's accomplished so far," he says.

The food truck was featured in CHEK News as a bridge between generations — and a way for Halah to give back to the community that welcomed her to Victoria.
