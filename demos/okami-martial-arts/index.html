狼 Okami — The Dojo

Crystal, Kelly, and Tal built this place on one idea: show up for your students.

Okami means wolf. Wolves train together, protect each other, and nobody gets left behind. That's the dojo.

Classes run evenings and weekends because this has always been about the work, not the business. Kids start young. Adults join later. Most of them stay.

Every age. Every level. One dojo.

Kids Martial Arts

Ages 4 and up. The kid who won't make eye contact in September is leading warmups by spring. Crystal and Kelly have watched it happen hundreds of times.

Adult Martial Arts

Olesya started over 9 years ago and has never felt judged. That's the room. You train at your level, and the people around you want you to improve.

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu

Technique-first ground work. Tal runs the rolling sessions where partners push each other without ego. You'll tap a lot. You'll learn faster because of it.

Muay Thai

Striking, clinch work, elbows, and knees. Taught the way it's meant to be taught: with respect for the art and the person learning it.
