Our Story

A Family Tradition, Now in Victoria

Puerto Vallarta Amigos is a family restaurant built on the concept of "casual food" — combining the speed and convenience of fast-casual with the authentic flavour and quality of traditional Mexican home cooking.

All of our dishes are based on old Espinoza family recipes which have proven to be a great success in the family's restaurants in Acapulco, Morelia, and Puerto Vallarta.

We pride ourselves on using the highest quality ingredients and preserving the originality that brings an authentic taste of Mexico right here to Victoria, BC.
