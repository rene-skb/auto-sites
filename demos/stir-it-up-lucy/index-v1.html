The Cook

From Saint Lucia to Yates Street.

Stir It Up is one woman's kitchen. She cooks everything herself, from scratch, the way she learned back home in Saint Lucia. The recipes are hers. The flavors are real.

The restaurant is tucked down an alley between Yates and Johnson. It's small. The menu changes depending on what she has. If you get there late, the oxtail is probably gone.

That's the kind of place this is.
