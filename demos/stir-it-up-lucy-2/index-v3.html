What to order

Five things. All of them good.

Made that morning. Every day, Tue–Sat.

Jerk Chicken

Scotch bonnet marinade, charred and slow. Served with rice and peas. This is the one people come back for.

Curry Goat

Cooked low and slow in a deep curry. The kind of flavor that takes hours to build and seconds to understand.

Patties

Flaky, golden, stuffed with spiced beef. Made fresh. Ask if they're still warm.

Rice & Peas

Coconut rice with kidney beans. Goes with everything. Often the best thing on the plate.

Featured

Roti Wraps

Fresh roti folded around curried fillings — chicken, potato, callaloo. Hand-rolled. A full meal, wrapped in St. Lucian tradition.

Our roots

St. Lucian cooking.
Victoria table.

St. Lucian cooking draws from French Creole, African, and Indian traditions. You taste it in the curry goat, the jerk rub, the coconut rice. Made from scratch, every day. No shortcuts.
