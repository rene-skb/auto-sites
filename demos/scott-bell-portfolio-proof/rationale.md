# Scott Bell Portfolio Proof — Rationale

## Slug
- `scott-bell-portfolio-proof`

## Intent
Build a portfolio-focused auto-sites demo that keeps the strongest content proof from `scott-bell-opus`, the chapter clarity from `scott-bell-portfolio-v8`, and the breadth/current-work framing from `scott-bell-v2`, without carrying over the AI-ish copy or the overly art-directed parts that started competing with the work.

## What was borrowed

### From `scott-bell-opus`
- Proof-led case study structure for Strike, AIOZ, Fountain, and KOHO
- Concrete metrics and credibility framing
- Screenshot-backed selected work instead of text-only claims
- Stronger professional weight overall

### From `scott-bell-portfolio-v8`
- Clear chapter pacing: current work, selected work, about, close
- `Scientist brain. Design hands.` framing for the about section
- `I'm most useful early.` as the closing posture
- Simpler sectioning that reads like a curated story, not a résumé dump

### From `scott-bell-v2`
- Hero opener: `24 products in 14 years. The last four, I built myself.`
- Breadth framing across Strike, AIOZ, Fountain, and KOHO
- Recent self-built products surfaced near the top
- More grounded sense of Scott as both designer and builder

## What changed
- Removed the AI-ish line from v8 (`I find the logic underneath...`) and replaced it with the stronger, cleaner opener from v2.
- Kept the chapter structure, but reduced the louder color-block treatment so the work proof stays in front.
- Strengthened AIOZ and Fountain so they no longer feel like supporting mentions under Strike.
- Restored KOHO as part of the career arc so the page shows more range.
- Reframed Nakamoto Design Co. as a studio built as a pipeline, which feels more specific and current.
- Kept copy short, factual, and grounded. No hype language, no mystical AI voice.

## Fact inventory used in copy
- `24 products in 14 years` → from `scott-bell-v2`
- Strike: `1.5M+ users`, `65+ countries`, `450+ screens`, `5 months` → from `scott-bell-opus` / `scott-bell-v2`
- AIOZ: `8 products`, `market cap from $13M to $1.38B`, `100× growth` → from `scott-bell-v2` / `scott-bell-opus`
- Fountain: `full rebuild in under five months`, `Apple featured twice`, semantic design system → from `scott-bell-opus` / `scott-bell-v2`
- KOHO: `employee #1`, `0 to 100K+ users`, `$800M+ valuation` → from `scott-bell-opus` / `scott-bell-v2`
- Nakamoto: `80+ site demos` / pipeline framing → from `scott-bell-v2` and existing portfolio demos
