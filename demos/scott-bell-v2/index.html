About

The person

I have a microbiology degree and an interaction design degree. Ten years of post-secondary. The micro taught me to look at complex systems and find structure. The design degree taught me to make that structure visible. I've been doing both ever since.

I've designed products since 2012. Apple featured my work twice. I was employee #1 at KOHO (now $800M+) and principal designer at AIOZ when it grew 100x. I taught UX at RED Academy. I host a radio show on Bside Radio called Dynamic Quality.

The work I care about most right now: building tools where designers and AI agents actually collaborate. LoopIn sends what I see in the browser straight to a coding agent. Clawprint gives agents real psychological profiles. Nakamoto Design Co. is 80+ sites I built with an AI pipeline I designed. This is where I want to work next.
