Story

Opened in March 2021, with a smart name and a room that gets the light right.

Ethan Choi and Zoey Jung opened URL Coffee in March 2021. Eater Seattle described the interior as “mid-century modern with a note of punkish whimsy,” which gets you close to the skateboard-deck stools and the soft, bright room in one sentence.

The name is the wink. The room is why people stay.

What to order

Start with the Walk to Work.

People mention this one by name: an iced latte with sweet cream and cacao dusted over the top.
