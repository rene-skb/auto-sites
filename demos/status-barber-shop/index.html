The Shop

The Music Is Half the Haircut.

Status has been on Yates Street for over 15 years. Walk through the door and you'll immediately know why 1,193 people have made the trip. The music hits first. Then the energy of a shop that genuinely loves what it does.

Tbone, John, and the crew are the kind of barbers who'll ask questions before they pick up the scissors. They read faces and hair types. The hot towel finish isn't an add-on — it's part of the standard.

"Atmosphere" is the word that appears in reviews more than any other. 105 times and counting. There's a reason people recommend this place to their whole family.
