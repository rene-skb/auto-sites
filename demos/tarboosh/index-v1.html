The name

A fez, a family,
and a food truck.

Halah Abouhassan's grandfather wore a tarboosh — a bright red fez — as part of his Egyptian military uniform. That hat is now painted on the side of her truck. The name isn't branding. It's her family.

Her son Basil is her partner in the business. Halah cooks everything. He handles everything else.

"Honestly, I'm just amazed with how much she's accomplished so far."

— Basil, on his mother Halah
