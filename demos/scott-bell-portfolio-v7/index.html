About

I studied microbiology before I studied design.

10 years of post-secondary. BSc in Microbiology, BA in Interaction Design. The scientific method never left. I still approach every problem the same way: observe, hypothesize, test, iterate.

14 years of product design after that. Fintech, bitcoin, podcasting, web3. Led design teams, shipped to millions of users.

Now I'm focused on the space between humans and AI. The tools I build help agents understand context, see what users see, and make decisions with more information.

Every system has logic underneath. My job is finding it and making it visible.

The logic underneath needs a designer.

Looking for product design roles in AI, agents, and human-machine collaboration.
