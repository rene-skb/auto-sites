About

Doug Doesn't Sell Clothes. He Introduces You to Them.

Walk in and he'll tell you where each piece came from. The WW2 jacket that crossed an ocean. The leather that softened over forty years. He knows the decade, the maker, the regiment.

Vintage Glory sits at Keenleyside and Thomas on Winnipeg's east side. Reviews call it a museum you can buy from. That's about right.

"Amiable Owner who takes pride in his product & establishment. Can easily see why this business is successful." 40 SAYERS · Via Google

The Name

The Clothes Outlasted the People Who Wore Them. That's the Point.

Doug picks every piece. Not for the label, not for the trend. For the thing that made someone keep it for forty years. That's what's on the rack.

The Collection

What You'll Find

01

The Jackets

Motorcycle leather. Bomber jackets. WW2 field coats. Doug knows where each one served. The leather's already broken in by someone who lived in it.

02

The History

Military insignia, regiment patches, pieces that crossed oceans. Not reproductions. The real thing, with the wear to prove it.

03

The Workwear

Denim that's faded honestly. Flannels with the right weight. Western shirts you won't find anywhere else in Winnipeg. Every piece hand-picked.

04

The Weird Stuff

Vintage glasses, antique accessories, things that defy categories. The pieces you didn't know existed until Doug pulled them off the shelf.
