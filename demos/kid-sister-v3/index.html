About

The flavors change when the fruit does.

Christen makes ice cream from scratch with organic dairy from the Island and fruit she sources locally. Blackberries from the hedgerows, figs from a neighbor's tree, strawberries roasted in the oven before they go into the churn.

Four flavors at a time. They rotate weekly. Some come back, some don't. The menu is on Instagram because it changes faster than a website can keep up.

The Name

She shows up with something sweet. That's what a kid sister does.

Kid Sister is on Esquimalt Road in Victoria. It's a small shop, and that's on purpose. Small means Christen can pick the fruit, make the ice cream, and scoop it herself. The whole thing, start to finish.
