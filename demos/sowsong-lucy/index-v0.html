In Dedication to Grandpa Soby

Where the name comes from

"Sow Song was a tune he'd sing to us grandkids most times we were with him. Playful and full of love."

Tia Noelle Soby grew up watching her grandfather play banjo in the garden. When it came time to name her jewelry brand, she chose the song he always sang — because the pieces she makes are meant to carry meaning the same way.

"When I make jewelry, I'm always thinking about connection. I want the pieces I make to hold meaning throughout our lifetime."

— Tia Noelle, Sow Song

The Maker

Tia Noelle Soby

Tia is a Vancouver Island jeweler who trained with local metalsmithing mentors before opening her studio in a converted Travelux trailer. Her previous life as a florist shows in the organic shapes she's drawn to — curved bezels, salt-and-pepper stones, gold that feels warm rather than precious.

She also does consultations in Tofino. Every piece is made one-on-one: a conversation first, then a design, then the ring you'll wear for the rest of your life.

How It Works

One ring, one conversation

Tia works with each client directly. No forms, no catalogue — just an email and a conversation about what you're looking for.

01

Reach out

Email Tia at tia@sowsong.com. Tell her what you're imagining — the occasion, any inspirations, whether you have heirloom pieces to incorporate.

02

Design together

She'll come back with a design that fits what you described. You talk through it, adjust if needed, and settle on something that feels right.

03

She makes it

Tia handcrafts the piece in her studio. When it's done, she'll get it to you — a ring that's yours and only yours.
