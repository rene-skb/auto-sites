Puff pastry, sausage rolls, and cakes worth crossing town for.

Barbershop Patisserie is Jill Barber's College Street bakery for lemon bichons, mille-feuille, savoury pastry, cookies, and cakes. It's open daily, built for takeout, and the case usually looks picked over by late afternoon for a reason.

What to get

Sweet, savoury, and very hard to leave with just one box.

Reviews keep circling the same things: hand-rolled puff pastry, the lemon bichon, sausage rolls, chicken pot pie, chocolate cake, bostock, and butterscotch apple mille-feuille. That's a strong kind of repetition.

Lemon bichon Tangy lemon curd inside caramelized, sugar-coated puff pastry. Angela A. called it her new favourite lemon treat.
Sausage rolls and savoury pastry The savoury side gets real love here. Ken T. singled out the seasoning in the sausage roll, and multiple writeups call it a crowd favourite.
Mille-feuille and cake Butterscotch apple mille-feuille has people ordering birthday cakes on purpose. The dark chocolate cake keeps showing up in reviews too.
Bostock, croissants, cookies Stanley S. praised the bostock, the croissant structure, and the chocolate chip cookie in the same visit. That's a useful signpost.
From the case

The name has swagger. The pastry backs it up.

Barber opened her own shop on a street she already walked every day. The result feels like a neighbourhood bakery with better instincts than the average bakery case.

Why this place feels different

“Everything that I make is something that I would eat.”

That line came straight from Jill Barber when she opened the shop. It explains the page better than any brand deck could.

She'd already built a reputation in Toronto pastry kitchens before taking over this Little Italy space and opening her own spot. Streets of Toronto describes the shop as open until pastries are sold out, which feels about right for a bakery built around puff pastry instead of convenience.
