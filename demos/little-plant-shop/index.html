Our Story
"The big trend right now is ordering everything in from overseas and you never know if they're faking the phytosanitary certificate or if it's stolen from the jungle. So we go local. Canadian grown."

Eric Gibson and Sara Gies met in high school, both went through the horticulture program at Olds College in Alberta, and have worked every kind of plant job imaginable. Eric was a grower at Edmonton's Muttart Conservatory. Sara ran the University of Alberta's research greenhouses — the cool ones on the rooftops that most people don't even know exist.

After a decade in Edmonton — from makers markets to a brick-and-mortar on Whyte Avenue to a bowler trailer doing mobile pop-ups — they took a year off, sold everything, and moved to Vancouver Island. They live out near Sooke now, surrounded by the Pacific Northwest wilderness they'd always wanted.

The shop opened on Johnson Street in October 2023, and they've been connecting with Victoria's plant community ever since. They carry the hardy stuff that does well everywhere, but they specialize in the things you won't see at a box store — rare tropicals, carnivorous plants, unusual specimens they propagate themselves.

The Name

Some Things Work Better
When They Don't Scale.

"Little" isn't a limitation — it's a decision. A space small enough that the owners know every plant on every shelf because they grew half of them. Two people, one room, and the things that thrive when you give them undivided attention.
