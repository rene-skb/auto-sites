Our Story

Run by volunteers. Stocked with care.

Kavod is Hebrew for honor. Every piece on these racks was sorted, priced, and placed by volunteers who believe clothing deserves a second life with someone who'll love it.

We'd rather show 50 great pieces than 500 forgettable ones. That's why the racks stay tight and the finds stay good.
