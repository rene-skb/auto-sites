The Story

Hopefully I Can Bring My Island to This Island

"Natalie is an amazing owner and very warm to all of her customers. The food is exquisite and breaks every bit of Caribbean essence to Victoria. Coming from a Trini, it is the real deal." — Ariel T. · Via Google

Natalie Justin moved to Victoria from Trinidad with a plan that fit in a carry-on: her family's recipes and the belief that a city surrounded by ocean deserved Caribbean food worth eating.

Stir It Up is her kitchen. She makes the roti dough by hand, rolls it fresh for every order, and fills it with slow-cooked curries and jerk that took years to perfect. No shortcuts. No assembly line. Just Natalie.

The Name

One Kitchen. One Cook. No Shortcuts.

People keep saying the food tastes like someone made it just for them. That's because someone did. There's no prep line, no sous chef, no timer going off in the back. Just Natalie, her recipes, and the time it takes to get it right.

The Food

What's Cooking

01

Jerk Chicken

Scotch bonnet, allspice, thyme. Grilled until the skin crisps and the inside stays tender. Served with rice and beans. The one everyone orders first.

02

Curry Chicken Roti

Hand-rolled roti skin, fried fresh while you wait. Filled with slow-cooked curry chicken, potato, dal, and Natalie's special spice blend.

03

Doubles

Two pieces of fried bara bread filled with curried chickpeas, tamarind sauce, and pepper sauce. A Trinidadian street food staple.

04

Jamaican Patties

Golden, flaky pastry. Seasoned beef. Baked fresh daily. The one you eat on the walk back to your car and wish you'd bought two more.
