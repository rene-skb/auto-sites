A pantry worth the trip. Five days a week.

The word

Dispensa.

In Italian, the dispensa is the pantry. The room where everything that matters to a cook lives. Not the refrigerator (that's the everyday). The dispensa is where you keep the things you've sought out.

The olive oil from the estate you trust. The aged cheese you drove somewhere to find. The pasta that actually fits the shape of the dish you're making. This shop is that room, on William Street.
