# Panel Notes — Payless Kitchen Cabinets

## Creative direction

Payless should feel like a practical Pasadena kitchen-remodel estimate desk, not a discount flyer. The useful differentiator is not another “dream kitchen” promise. It is decision clarity: public price ranges, free consultation, written estimate, licensed/insured crew, and real official project/team imagery.

Hero pattern: `image-top-text-below`  
Typography: Lora + Manrope  
Review layout: no customer review cards; same-treatment factual proof rows and project-gallery proof because compliant named review quotes were not verified  
Visit/contact layout: final consultation desk with call CTA, Monday-Saturday consultation note, service area, and what to have ready  
Stats bar style: no-stats

## WHY critique

### Steve Jobs — 8.0
- The strongest idea is “a quote you can actually read.” That is more ownable than generic remodeling beauty.
- The page should lead with the finished-room image, then snap immediately to the money/process promise.
- Keep the discount language out of the hero. It cheapens a high-ticket decision.

### Paul Graham — 7.8
- The facts are good, but v0 still has too much remodel-language gravity.
- Cut any claim that sounds like a contractor could swap in their own name.
- Price ranges are the sharpest information. Make them feel useful, not scary.

### David Ogilvy — 8.1
- “A kitchen quote you can actually read” is specific and credible.
- The public cost ranges are the ad. Put them where people can find them.
- Do not invent review quotes. If the names/quotes are not verified, use factual proof instead.

**WHY average:** 7.97

**Next changes**
1. Tighten the hero from broad remodel aspiration to estimate clarity.
2. Add compact official project proof so the page is not only pricing/process.
3. Keep the CTA literal: call the phone number, see price ranges.
4. Remove promotional “save” framing from visible copy.

## WHAT critique

### Don Norman — 8.1
- The structure answers the right homeowner questions: scope, cost, process, trust, contact.
- Pricing ranges need explicit context that final quotes depend on material, labor, and site conditions.
- The contact panel should say what to prepare before the consultation.

### Steve Krug — 8.0
- The page is scannable. Section labels and price rows make the high-ticket information easy to skim.
- Avoid repeating the phone number in too many places. One hero CTA and final CTA are enough.
- Service cards are clear, but keep their descriptions short.

### Jakob Nielsen — 7.9
- Mobile price rows must wrap cleanly because dollar ranges are long.
- The dark section contrast is acceptable if secondary text stays warm and large enough.
- Avoid generic map links. No named business-pin URL was verified, so omit directions.

**WHAT average:** 8.00

**Next changes**
1. Add price-range disclaimers directly in the pricing section.
2. Keep phone CTAs to hero and final contact section.
3. Check mobile wrapping for long ranges.
4. Omit map/directions rather than using a generic map query.

## HOW critique

### Massimo Vignelli — 8.0
- The single-column image-first hero gives the site calm authority.
- Reduce decorative card language. Factual rows are more elegant than another proof-card grid.
- The palette works because clay red is an accent, not a theme park.

### Erik Spiekermann — 8.1
- Lora has enough warmth for the home category without sliding into wedding/editorial cliché.
- Manrope body text is clean and readable. Keep body weight at 400+.
- Price rows need strict alignment and no tiny metadata.

### Dieter Rams — 8.2
- The page earns its content because each section answers a different question.
- The gallery is compact. Do not add a second image moment.
- Remove anything that behaves like a stats bar in disguise.

### Emil Kowalski — 8.2
- Motion should be only scroll reveal and button feedback.
- Use custom easing, stagger lightly, and respect reduced motion.
- No hover zoom on static images.

**HOW average:** 8.13

**Next changes**
1. Replace any extra proof-card clusters with restrained proof rows.
2. Implement scroll reveals and button active states only.
3. Keep images static unless linked.
4. Verify mobile hero height and price row wrapping after motion.
