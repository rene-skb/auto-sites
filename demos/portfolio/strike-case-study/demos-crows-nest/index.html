How It Works

From idea to ink.

01
Consultation

Walk in or call ahead. Share your vision, placement, and size. We'll match you with the right artist for your style.

02
Design

Your artist creates a custom design based on your ideas. Revisions happen until it feels exactly right.

03
Session

Relax in a clean, comfortable space. Your artist works with care and precision in a fully sanitary environment.

04
Aftercare

Leave with thorough aftercare instructions and confidence in the healing process. We're here for follow-up questions.
