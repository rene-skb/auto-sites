Open four days a week. The bread doesn't last that long.

Victoria, BC · Thursday to Sunday

Three stools, a counter, and a line out the door.

Working Culture bakes sourdough, pastries, and sandwiches at 2716 Rock Bay Avenue in Victoria. Four days a week, Thursday through Sunday. Everything by hand, everything from scratch.

The cinnamon raisin shows up on Sundays. The olive and lemon zest batard shows up when it shows up. If you're looking for a menu that stays the same every week, this probably isn't your spot.
