The place

Small room. Bright walls. Lunch that moves fast.

That is really all most people need to know. It is on Yates, the entrance is in the alley, and once you have been once you will not miss it again.

Stir It Up looks like the kind of place somebody tells you about, not the kind of place that buys ads.

Painted walls. Hand-done sign. Food that already has people talking. That is enough.

188 people on Google, 4.5 stars, and no big sales pitch anywhere in sight.
