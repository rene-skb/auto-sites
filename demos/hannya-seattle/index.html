The Name

The Hannya mask is the face of a jealous woman transformed.

The Hannya (般若) is the Japanese Noh theatre mask of a jealous female demon, one of the oldest subjects in irezumi tattooing. The studio took the name on purpose.

On 4th Ave in Belltown. You book ahead. You leave feeling like something got done right. Gallery-quiet space, soothing music, total focus on the work.

The Work

Irezumi

Japanese traditional

Bold linework, solid fills, classic subjects. The irezumi tradition runs through every piece done here.

Fine line

Minimalist & realism

The artists here work in fine line and realism alongside the traditional work. See @hannyaseattle for current portfolios and to book.
