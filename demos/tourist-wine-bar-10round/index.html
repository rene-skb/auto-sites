About
"You're never supposed to know everything. You're just supposed to stay open."

Tourist is named for the right way to approach wine. Not as an expert, not as a collector — as someone who is genuinely curious about where they are and what's in the glass.

We opened on Blanshard with small plates, an honest wine list, and the Sunday Sandwich — which changes every week and has become something regulars plan their week around.

The list is built around producers who farm honestly, intervene minimally, and bottle something that tastes like where it came from.

The Wine

We believe wine should taste like where it came from — not like a process. Our list is built around makers who farm honestly, intervene minimally, and bottle something alive.

The list changes often. Some bottles stay a season. Some become regulars. All of them made us say "you should try this" before they made it on the menu.
