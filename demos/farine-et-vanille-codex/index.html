The Bakery

Flour and Vanilla. That's the Foundation.

Farine & Vanille is an artisan bakery in the heart of Mile-End, one of Montréal's most food-obsessed neighbourhoods. Every pastry is built on French technique and quality ingredients, laminated by hand, and baked fresh before the neighbourhood wakes up.

The croissants are the draw. But regulars know the seasonal cream pastries with freeze-dried fruit, the ganache cookies, and the galette des rois are just as worth the trip.

The Name

Farine Means Flour. Vanille Means Vanilla.

Two ingredients. The simplest ones. The ones that disappear into everything but hold it all together. That's the whole philosophy.

From the Oven

What We Bake

Croissants

Classic butter croissants, laminated by hand. The golden layers and shattering crust that keep the line moving every morning.

Cream Pastries

Seasonal fillings with freeze-dried fruit. Raspberry cream, strawberry, whatever the season demands. Gone by noon.

Ganache Cookies

Dark chocolate ganache dome, dusted with freeze-dried raspberry. Crisp shell, soft centre. Not a cookie you've had before.

Galette des Rois

Puff pastry with almond frangipane, scored by hand. A French tradition made right, available seasonally.

Pain au Chocolat

Same lamination as the croissants, with two bars of dark chocolate tucked inside. Warm from the oven is the move.

Seasonal Specials

The menu rotates. Follow @farineetvanille on Instagram for what's fresh this week.
