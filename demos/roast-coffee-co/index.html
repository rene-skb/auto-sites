The Roastery

Fewer beans, more attention.

Most roasters blend to create consistency. We don't. Every bag is a single origin, roasted in small batches the same week it ships. You taste the farm, the altitude, the season.

Our menu rotates. When a harvest is gone, it's gone. That's not scarcity marketing. That's how coffee actually works when you pay attention to it.
