The Family

From Nafpaktos to Cook Street

"Our Greek food truck is truly one-of-a-kind. We proudly serve authentic dishes made from my grandma's cherished recipes, offering a taste of genuine Greek cuisine." — Eirini S., Founder

Eirini and her dad Jerry opened the first truck. Then Anna and Christo joined. Then mum Martha. Now three trucks roam Vancouver Island, and the family still cooks together every day.

The recipes are from Nafpaktos, a port town on the Gulf of Corinth. Grandma's spanakopita. Her baklava. The souvlaki marinade she never wrote down but everyone remembers.

The Name

Five People. Three Trucks. One Grandmother's Kitchen.

Every recipe came across the ocean in someone's memory. Nothing was written down. Everything was passed on.

The Food

Grandma's Menu

01

Souvlaki Wraps · $14–16

Chicken, lamb, or falafel in warm pita with tzatziki. The thing everyone orders first. The thing everyone orders again.

02

Calamari Wrap · $16

Crispy calamari in warm pita. This is the one that turns first-timers into regulars. "10/10" appears in the reviews more than any other phrase.

03

Skewer Boxes · $16–20

Souvlaki skewers with rice, Greek salad, pita. The regulars get the meat platter with lamb and don't share.

04

Spanakopita & Baklava · $4–6

Grandma's flaky spinach pie and honey-walnut baklava. Plus bougatsa — the custard pastry nobody expects from a food truck.
