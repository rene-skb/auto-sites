About

Your neighbourhood barbershop

Brothers Barbershop has been a Victoria staple for over 20 years. We started with a simple idea: give great haircuts at fair prices in a place that feels like home.

Our barbers, including longtime favourites Dennis and Khan, bring decades of combined experience. We're walk-in only because we think getting a haircut should be easy, not a scheduling exercise. Grab a coffee, sit down, and we'll take care of the rest.

We're family-friendly, dog-friendly (especially at our Langford shop), and we treat every customer like a regular. That's not a slogan. That's just how we do things.
