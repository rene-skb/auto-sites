# Bakerbots Baking — Panel Notes

## WHY critique

**Steve Jobs — 7.5**
- The appetite-first direction is right. The strongest move is treating the bakery case as the product instead of trying to invent lore.
- The hero needed more inevitability. Lead with the reason to go, not a category list.

**Paul Graham — 7.0**
- Some copy still sounded slightly explained instead of observed. Better once the language got shorter and more physical.
- The core idea is simple: this is a place with too many good dessert options. Say that more directly.

**David Ogilvy — 7.5**
- "Dessert worth the Bloor detour" is a stronger headline than the original list-led line.
- The proof section works when it uses real editorial language instead of brand adjectives.

**Panel average:** 7.33

**Next changes**
- Replace the list-like hero with a conviction line.
- Tighten the proof section so each item says one useful thing.
- Make the visit section answer practical questions faster.

## WHAT critique

**Don Norman — 7.5**
- The path is clear: understand the place, look at the food, get the visit details. Good.
- Section labels help, but a few headings needed to say what the user gets more explicitly.

**Steve Krug — 7.5**
- Easy to scan overall. The gallery section got clearer once the heading became more literal.
- A couple of body paragraphs were still a sentence too long.

**Jakob Nielsen — 7.0**
- Useful info is present, but the visit intro originally wasted a little time. The tighter version fixes that.
- Labels and CTA language are now clearer and more consistent.

**Panel average:** 7.33

**Next changes**
- Tighten headings so visitors understand each section instantly.
- Cut softer explanatory copy.
- Keep utility labels and CTA wording blunt and specific.

## HOW critique

**Massimo Vignelli — 7.0**
- The type pairing is warm and appropriate. The system needed slightly firmer rhythm and contrast in the cards.
- Better once the softer backgrounds were tightened.

**Erik Spiekermann — 7.5**
- The serif does good brand work. Body copy became stronger after removing decorative phrasing.
- Utility sections read better once the contrast improved.

**Dieter Rams — 7.0**
- The right things are on the page. Reduction helped.
- Keep resisting extra sections. This business does not need them.

**Emil Kowalski — 7.5**
- Scroll reveal is enough here. Anything louder would be fake gloss.
- Motion should stay on entrances and button feedback only.

**Panel average:** 7.25

**Next changes**
- Increase card contrast and visual discipline.
- Keep the page short.
- Add restrained reveal motion and no decorative hover effects.
