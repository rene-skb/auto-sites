Our Story

Four restaurants taught us everything. This one brings it all together.

Clark Deutscher has been cooking in Victoria for over a decade, building and closing and rebuilding restaurants because that's what chefs do when they care more about the food than the formula. Hanks. Nowhere. Ate. Somewhere. Each one a chapter, each one a lesson.

Unicorn Sparkles is the distillation. An intimate courtyard space at Sussex Place where the menu changes with the seasons, the farmers, and whatever Chase and Keith are inspired by that week. Tasting menus. Family feasts. The occasional pizza night. Always local, always intentional, always a little unexpected.

The name? It's a reminder not to take ourselves too seriously. The food does the serious work.

The Experience

Trust the kitchen. Enjoy the ride.

01

Tasting Menu

A multi-course journey through whatever's freshest and most exciting. Sit at the chef's bar for two and watch it come together, or gather around the table for the full family-style feast. Menus start around $65 per person.

02

Hyper-Local

Ingredients from Vancouver Island farms and purveyors, prepared with techniques earned across four restaurant kitchens. The menu reads like a love letter to the region.

03

Intimate Setting

Tucked into a courtyard off Douglas Street, seating for about two dozen. No pretension, no dress code. Just great food in a space that feels like a well-kept secret.
