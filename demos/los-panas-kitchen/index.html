The Food

Comfort food that crossed an ocean

Golden corn dough, shaped by hand, cooked until crisp outside, pillowy inside. Split open and stuffed with slow-pulled pork, black beans, sweet plantain, fresh cheese. That's an arepa.

Everything here is gluten-free by nature. The corn dough. The fried plantain patacones. The guasacaca sauce made fresh daily. No substitutions needed. Just real Venezuelan food.

What does "Panas" mean?

"Pana" is Venezuelan slang for buddy, pal, your people. Los Panas means the crew.

When you eat here, you're part of it.

Our Story

Three years from "what's an arepa?" to feeding festivals

Carlos Bermudez moved from Venezuela to Victoria with a dream and a recipe. He started Los Panas Kitchen to share the food he grew up with: the same arepas his family made, the same slow-pulled pork, the same guasacaca sauce.

Three years later, he can barely make enough to keep up. Follow the truck at Market Square in summer, or grab a frozen pack from Market on Yates when you can't wait.
