How It Works

A conversation, then a ring.

Start with an email. Tell Tia what you're imagining — the occasion, any stones or metals in mind, whether you have heirloom pieces to incorporate. She'll write back.

She designs something specific to you. You talk through it, adjust as needed, and settle on something that feels right before she starts making it.

Tia works with engagement rings, wedding bands, memorial pieces, and heirloom remakes. Based in Nanaimo, with consultations available in Tofino.

Custom pieces take time. Most commissions run 8–12 weeks. Worth mentioning your timeline when you reach out.

The Maker

Made by Tia

Tia works from a converted trailer studio in Nanaimo. Warm yellow gold, salt-and-pepper diamonds, organic shapes. Every piece starts with a conversation about who it's for and what moment it marks.

The name comes from a song her Grandpa Soby sang on banjo — playful and full of love. The pieces carry the same meaning.
