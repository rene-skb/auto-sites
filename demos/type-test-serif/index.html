The Name

"Wairua means your spirit. We wanted a place where people can nurture their spirit and just connect — either with yourself or others."
— Wilson North, Co-founder

Our Story

A dream born during a pandemic

Wilson and Hanisa North always knew they wanted to work together, work for themselves. The shape of that dream became clear during the quiet of COVID — a coffee shop was their destiny.

Wilson is from New Zealand, and brought with him a Māori philosophy of spirit and connection. Hanisa brought the drive to build something real. Together they created WAIRUA — a place where the community could gather, slow down, and nurture something quiet inside themselves.

They opened in late March 2025, expecting a slow start. The North Park community had other plans — the support, they say, has been heartwarming.
