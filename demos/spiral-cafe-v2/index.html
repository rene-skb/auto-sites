Our Story

The neighbourhood's
living room

Spiral Cafe is a family-run coffee shop in the heart of Vic West, housed in a character-filled old building with big windows and a warmth you feel the moment you walk in.

We serve organic, fair-trade coffee alongside baked goods from Mosi's Bakery. Local artists showcase their work on our walls each month, and on select evenings, the cafe fills with live music — no cover, just community.

"That yellow wall and hand-lettered menu board, so warm and welcoming. It feels like a little corner of the world where time slows down."
