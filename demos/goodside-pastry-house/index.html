The Story

They Had Every Reason to Stay in Vancouver. They Didn't.

After a decade running the stations in Vancouver's high-pressure kitchens, Haley Landa and Curtis Helm arrived in Victoria in 2020 with one goal: to build something that belonged to a neighborhood, not a dining trend.

Their flour is organic. Their eggs are from Lockwood Farms. Every month, they throw out the menu and start again — not because the old one wasn't good, but because staying creative is how they stay honest.

"We thought the newness would wear off. It never did. We're really, really grateful that after two-and-a-half years people are still willing to line up." — Haley Landa, Co-Owner · CHEK News

The Bakes

These Are the Kinds of Things You'll Find.

01

The Croissant

The one that started the line. Organic flour, Lockwood Farms eggs, properly laminated. It's the baseline — and it's as good as any you'd find in a Paris arrondissement.

02

The Rotating Menu

Passionfruit brioche bombs. Lychee-raspberry cheesecake mousse. Coffee and dulcey tarts. Whatever's on the website right now is this month's menu. Next month it won't exist.

03

Custom Cakes

4" cakes (serves 2–3) from $21. 6" cakes (serves 6–8) and up. Order through the website — same French technique, just with your occasion in mind.
