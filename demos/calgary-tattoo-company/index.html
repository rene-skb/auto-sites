What the shop says

Built for people who want the practical stuff answered before they sit down.

The official FAQ is unusually useful. Instead of vague studio talk, it tells you exactly how walk-ins work, what the minimums are, how deposits behave, and what to do after the appointment.

Walk-ins

First come, first served.

Walk-ins are welcome. If the team cannot get you in that day, they will book you an appointment instead.

Pricing

Minimums are explicit.

$120 plus tax for 15 minutes or less with one needle, $150 plus tax for 15 to 45 minutes, then each artist’s hourly rate plus a $40 shop fee beyond that.

Piercings

Regular piercings start at $42.

The home page lists piercings at $42, and the FAQ says they handle most regular nose, ear, lip, and belly button piercings, but not dermals.

Before you book

Sleep, eat, show up clean, and know how deposits work.

A lot of tattoo sites force you to hunt for this stuff. Here, the FAQ does the heavy lifting, so the page should too.

Prep

Come fed and rested.

The shop recommends a good night’s sleep, a solid meal, a shower, and lotioning the area for a week or two before the appointment, but not on the day of.

Deposits

Cash deposits hold the slot.

Deposits are $100 for one to three hours and $300 for four hours and up, per day. Three days’ notice keeps the reschedule open. Less than that counts as a cancellation.

Aftercare

Traditional heal or second skin, both spelled out.

The FAQ gives both paths directly: wash gently with cool water and unscented soap for a traditional heal, or leave second skin on for four to five days unless it leaks or irritates.

Safety

Single-use gear, Cavicide wipe-downs, and HEPA filtration.

The studio says all components besides wash bottles and tattoo machines are single-use and pre-sterilized, with each station cleaned after every tattoo.
