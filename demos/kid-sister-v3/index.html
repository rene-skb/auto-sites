Our Story

A little shop with a lot of flowers.

Kid Sister started as a tiny spot in Fan Tan Alley and grew into Esquimalt's favourite neighbourhood scoop shop. Everything is made from scratch in small batches — organic dairy, local fruit, seasonal everything.

We don't do soft serve. We don't do factory ice cream. We scoop by hand, surrounded by fresh flowers, and when a flavor's gone, it's gone. Come early.
