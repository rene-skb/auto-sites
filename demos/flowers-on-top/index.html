Our Story

Thirty-Five Years of Getting It Right.

Flowers On Top began in 1990 as a flower cart at Fort and Government Street. The name came naturally — we put flowers on top of restaurant tables. People kept asking where to get them.

Now working from our North Park warehouse, we go to the United Flower Growers auction in Burnaby every single week — sourcing from BC growers in the Fraser Valley and Vancouver Island so your arrangement arrives at its peak.

A family business entering its second generation. Simone and Jacqueline are here, and they're answering the phone when you call.
