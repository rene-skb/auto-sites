# Panel notes — Dark Age Tattoo Studio

## WHY critique

### Steve Jobs — 7.5
- The page feels premium and credible right away.
- The idea is still too broad. It says "good tattoo studio" more than it says "this is why Dark Age exists."
- Pick one action and one conviction above the fold.

### Paul Graham — 8.0
- The page is grounded in real facts and real work. Good.
- The thesis needs sanding down. Too many sections are making the same trust argument in slightly different words.
- Say the simplest true thing about why this studio is useful.

### David Ogilvy — 8.5
- Strong materials: real work, real awards, real location, real phone.
- The copy can sell harder by being more specific, especially in the hero.
- The main CTA needs to win clearly over the secondary ones.

**Panel average:** 8.00

**Next changes for Lucy**
1. Sharpen the hero into one clearer promise built around the multi-artist advantage.
2. Reduce repeat trust messaging across hero and proof sections.
3. Make one primary CTA dominant and push the others into support roles.

## WHAT critique

### Don Norman — 4.0
- The mood is strong, but the page still makes visitors work too hard to find the practical path.
- Key decision info should surface faster and in more compact formats.

### Steve Krug — 5.0
- Good taste, but not obvious enough on first glance.
- The page needs shorter copy, clearer action priority, and easier scan points.

### Jakob Nielsen — 4.5
- Credibility is present, but friction is still too high for a first-time visitor.
- Put contact and decision cues earlier, then compress the proof into faster-reading modules.

**Panel average:** 4.50

**Next changes for Lucy**
1. Compress the hero into a shorter what, where, why structure.
2. Bring practical visit and contact cues earlier in the page.
3. Turn the proof block into faster-scanning utility cards instead of a longer narrative.

## HOW critique

### Massimo Vignelli — 7.5
- Strong disciplined structure, but the hero over-extends vertically and leans too hard on asymmetry.
- Headline breaks feel slightly theatrical; tighten line rhythm.

### Erik Spiekermann — 7.0
- Good typographic ambition. Serif display + restrained sans works.
- Spacing and alignment rhythm needs to be more consistent section-to-section.
- Small labels and body text on dark backgrounds are borderline on legibility.

### Dieter Rams — 7.0
- The page is honest. All the important things are present.
- Reduce ornament: heavy shadows and rounded-corner cards everywhere add mood but not clarity.

### Emil Kowalski — 6.5
- Motion is implied but not built. The page risks static without connective reveals.
- Transitions should be minimal, quiet, and tactile. No gimmicks competing with the artwork.

**Panel average:** 7.00

**Next changes for Lucy**
1. Tighten hero headline — fix the line breaks and reduce theatricality.
2. Improve legibility: raise body text contrast, standardize vertical spacing.
3. Add restrained scroll-reveal motion and gentle hover states; strip excess shadow.
