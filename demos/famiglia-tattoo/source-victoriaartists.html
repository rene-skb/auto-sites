ARTIST BIOS

Paige Parker (she/they)

@_paigeparker_

paigeparkerillustration.ca

newsletter sign-up

Paige is a west coast-based multidisciplinary artist, with a focus on flora and fauna. Her main media are tattooing and digital illustration, while her peripheral practices include oil painting and film photography. Paige lives with her two rescue rabbits on unceded Lekwungen territory.

MEAGAN BERLIN (they/them)

@berlin.tattoos

berlintattoos.com

Meagan Berlin is a white queer person living on Lək̓ʷəŋən territories. Meagan’s illustration practice is based on transcribing personal moments, objects, and sleeping dreams. The collaborative surprise of every tattoo process—especially figuring out placement and composition—is their favourite part of tattooing. Meagan hopes to continue to be asked into large-scale projects (especially backpieces!) and the relational trust of working on memorial pieces.

MADDY VALLEE (she/they)

@_mostlynever_

booking form

Maddy is a self-taught illustrator and tattoo mentee of Cassandra Faire. Their work cannot exist without the land and waters of Vancouver Island, particularly inspired by the remote, boat-in only communities they have spent their childhood growing up in, and adulthood revisiting. Embracing life, death, growth, and decay of the un-sanitized natural world; her favourite projects work within these holistic themes and the personal connections we have with them. Attempting to bridge the gap between the concept of nature and humans as separate, each tattoo is a reminder of those we love and share the world with.

THEA (they/them)

@buglette.tats

custom booking form

flash booking form

Thea is a queer/ indigenous/settler artist, grown in Secwepemc lands where they apprenticed in 2018. Thea’s work focuses on following the movement of body, mapping out and draping on the shapes that flow with you - be it with ornaments inspired by their heritage, free-handed florals, or textural animals, mixing all these components together. Working with the ideology that Tattoo Is Medicine, they are always striving towards a practice that embodies safety, humility and respect.

Caroline (she/they)

@novemberbluesart

booking form

Caroline takes her inspiration from nature, magic, poetry and adventures. She uses negative space and bold lines to create unique and poetic tattoos. Thanks to her graphic design background, she’s got a keen eye for composition and enjoys playing around with placement, patterns, and design variation. Caroline is a people person; she loves to connect with her clients and peers through art and shared values. She’s kind and caring and thrives in creating an environment where all folks can feel welcome and comfortable.

Robin Graves (she/they)

@robingravesstudio
robingraves.net
newsletter sign up

Robin is a self taught illustrator and tattooist practicing their craft in Victoria BC, on Lkwungen Lands. Their work evokes a playful darkness inspired by a wide range of character archetypes, mixing established mythology with contemporary storytelling. Her flash pieces draw heavily from medieval etchings & gothic surrealism. When not tattooing, she will likely be found with a hot cup of tea, either queuing up a film or about town with a novel in tow.
