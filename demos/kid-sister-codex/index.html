The Source

The Fruit Comes First. Everything Else Follows.

"The best ice cream starts in the field, not the kitchen. If the strawberries are perfect, you don't need much else."

Organic dairy from the island. Blackberries foraged from local hedgerows. Figs picked by hand. Every flavour starts with what's ripe right now.

The menu rotates weekly because the seasons do. When it's gone, it's gone. Instagram is the real-time menu.
