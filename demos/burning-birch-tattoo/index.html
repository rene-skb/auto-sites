Your tattoo deserves a quiet room.

Burning Birch is a private tattoo studio in Calgary's Marda Loop. No walk-ins, no rush, no audience. Just you, your artist, and the work.

Private Studio Marda Loop, Calgary By Appointment
The Studio

Not a shop. A studio.

Burning Birch is what happens when you strip away everything a tattoo parlor doesn't need. No neon signs, no walk-in traffic, no waiting room TV. Just a clean, quiet space on 34th Avenue where two artists do their best work.

Melissa and Janelle built this space to work the way they want — with intention, without interruption. Every appointment gets their full attention.

How It Works

One client at a time.

DM your idea on Instagram — reference photos, placement, size. Your artist replies with a quote and books your session. No deposit until you approve the design.

When you arrive, the studio is yours. No other clients, no background noise. Just a conversation about what you want and an artist with nowhere else to be.
