# Panel Notes — Certified Service

Built 2026-05-13 by Lucy on `openai-codex/gpt-5.5`.

## Discovery summary

Certified Service is not a no-website rescue. It has an official content-heavy WordPress site with many service-area/service pages, a contact form, phone CTA, Carrier/NATE/licensing proof, and extensive maintenance/commercial detail. The opportunity is compression and conversion clarity: make the first screen answer “who should I call and why trust them?” without forcing the visitor through an SEO page maze.

Key sourced strengths:
- Burbank HVAC service company.
- Family-owned / three generations / over 40 years.
- NATE certified technicians.
- Service techs not paid on commission.
- Carrier Factory Authorized Dealer.
- CA C-10 & C-20 License #421428, bonded and insured.
- Free replacement estimates.
- Maintenance agreements and light commercial HVAC.

## WHY panel

Reviewers: Steve Jobs, Paul Graham, David Ogilvy  
Average: **8.0**

- Jobs: Strongest line is “Comfort fixed without the sales pressure.” It turns the no-commission fact into the emotional reason to call. The page feels more like a competent dispatcher than an HVAC brochure.
- Graham: Good compression. The site’s actual value is not “we offer every HVAC thing.” It is trust during a moment when the customer is hot, cold, or worried about cost.
- Ogilvy: The no-commission claim should stay high. It is specific, credible, and saleable. Credentials are better than generic “honest reliable service” copy.

Changes applied:
- Hero led with no-sales-pressure positioning.
- No-commission proof placed in quick strip and full proof section.
- Avoided fake reviews and invented emotional storytelling.

## WHAT panel

Reviewers: Don Norman, Steve Krug, Jakob Nielsen  
Average: **8.1**

- Norman: Navigation maps to customer intent: Services, Trust, Maintenance, Contact. Good phone-first behavior.
- Krug: Hero answers what/where/action quickly. Quick strip and service cards make the site scannable.
- Nielsen: Single-page format cannot replace full service-area SEO, but it works as a campaign landing page. Contact details and map listing are findable.

Changes applied:
- Repeated phone CTA in hero, services, maintenance, and contact.
- Kept service cards broad enough to reduce overload.
- Added area list without dumping every city from the official site.

## HOW panel

Reviewers: Massimo Vignelli, Erik Spiekermann, Dieter Rams, Emil Kowalski  
Average: **8.0**

- Vignelli: Grid discipline is solid. Navy/cream/copper has service-industry authority without looking like a template.
- Spiekermann: Fraunces gives weight but is not overused as italic decoration. Body text has adequate contrast.
- Rams: Proof cards are purposeful. No stats bar, no fake testimonial section, no decorative excess.
- Kowalski: Motion is appropriate: reveal and button feedback only. No image hover zoom. Reduced-motion covered.

Changes applied:
- Used consistent proof-card treatment, but did not call them testimonials.
- Kept motion quiet and utilitarian.
- Added OG tags and sources file.

## Self-review

Score: **8.2**

Strongest parts:
